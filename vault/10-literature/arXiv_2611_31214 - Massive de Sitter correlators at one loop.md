# Massive de Sitter correlators at one loop

## Abstract

> Fermions and massive fields charged under an unbroken symmetry couple to the inflaton only in
> pairs, so their signals in primordial correlators begin at one loop. Templates for these signals
> are mostly late-time estimates. Starting from a representation of massive de Sitter correlators
> as finite Mellin-Barnes integrals, we compute the exact squeezed coefficients of these signals
> and their corrections away from the squeezed limit. The leading coefficient factorizes into a
> hard part and a soft vertex. Late-time estimates treat the hard part exactly but approximate the
> soft vertex by its late-time limit, whereas we compute both exactly. Without a chemical
> potential, the exact coefficient differs from the late-time one by a universal factor that
> depends only on the masses in the loop and the coupling at the soft vertex. For the heavy
> Standard Model loops, the exact signals are ten to thirty times larger than their late-time
> estimates. With a chemical potential, the ratio depends on the model, and at our benchmarks it
> reaches a factor of thirty for helical vectors and about a hundred for the fermion triangle,
> where we agree with recent exact results. Away from the squeezed limit the leading term soon
> fails, mainly through a drift in the phase of the oscillation, which we derive for scalar and
> fermion loops.
