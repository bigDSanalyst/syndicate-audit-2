# Average lower bound on magnetic double-well tunneling with asymptotically large well separation

## Abstract

> We study tunneling between two identical, smooth, compactly supported potential wells in a
> constant perpendicular magnetic field, with the coupling and magnetic field fixed and the well
> separation tending to infinity. For a sufficiently small positive magnetic field, we prove lower
> bounds on the hopping coefficient and on the splitting of the two lowest eigenvalues outside a
> set of separations of density zero. If the well centers are separated by $2d_1$ and the magnetic
> field strength is $bλ$, the lower bounds have the form $e^{-bλd_1^2-Cd_1^{1+δ}}$ for every fixed
> $δ\in(0,1)$. No radial or reflection symmetry of the single-well potential is assumed. The proof
> combines the explicit Landau resolvent kernel with a complex analytic estimate for logarithmic
> averages of the hopping coefficient.
