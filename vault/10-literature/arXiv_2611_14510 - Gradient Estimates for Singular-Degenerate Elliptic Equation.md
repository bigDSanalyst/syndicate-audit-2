# Gradient Estimates for Singular-Degenerate Elliptic Equations with Solution-Dependent Coefficients

## Abstract

> We study a conormal boundary value problem for a class of singular-degenerate quasilinear
> elliptic equations in divergence form in bounded domains. The leading terms are measurable in
> the spatial variable and depend nonlinearly on both the solution and its gradient. The
> singularity or degeneracy is governed by a power of the distance to the domain boundary, with
> exponent greater than negative one. In particular, the corresponding weights need not belong to
> the Muckenhoupt $A_2$ class. Assuming that the weighted mean oscillations of the leading terms
> in the spatial variable are sufficiently small on small balls and that the domains have
> Lipschitz boundaries with sufficiently small Lipschitz constants, we establish global weighted
> $W^{1,p}$-estimates for weak solutions with bounded weighted mean oscillation. Our results
> extend recent estimates for equations whose leading terms are independent of the solution
> itself.
