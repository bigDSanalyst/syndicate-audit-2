# Polarization signatures of mixed scalar - pseudoscalar ALP - photon couplings in intense laser pulses

## Abstract

> We investigate photon-to-axion-like-particle (ALP) conversion in a finite, linearly polarized
> laser pulse for a single ALP field with simultaneous scalar-type and pseudoscalar-type couplings
> to photons. Varying the collision angle between the strong pulse and the probe provides a
> continuous kinematic scan of the resonant ALP mass. For fixed kinematics in this setting, the
> scalar-type and pseudoscalar-type ALP conversion amplitudes interfere coherently, making both
> the photon-to-ALP conversion probability and the polarization rotation depend on the difference
> between the probe polarization angle $α$ and the coupling angle $θ_g$. Conversion is maximal at
> $|α-θ_g|=90^\circ$, where the polarization rotation vanishes, while the rotation magnitude peaks
> at $α-θ_g=45^\circ$ modulo $90^\circ$, where conversion is half-maximal. These complementary
> polarization-angle dependences provide polarization-resolved signatures of mixed scalar--
> pseudoscalar photon couplings.
