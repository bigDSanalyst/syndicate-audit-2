# Deterministic nonlinear domain engineering algorithm with variable sub-coherence-length domains

## Abstract

> We present a new and improved deterministic algorithm that calculates the poling domains for the
> implementation of arbitrary phase-matching functions (PMF) in quasi-phase-matched nonlinear
> crystals for parametric down-conversion photon sources. In contrast to previous work, the
> algorithm presented here makes full use of the limits of the fabrication process, such as the
> minimum feature size and resolution of the lithographic process underpinning the poling
> procedure. This minimizes the tracking error to the target electric field and improves the
> accuracy in reaching the desired PMF. We give a tutorial on how to calculate the target function
> for arbitrary PMFs and run the algorithm. We also demonstrate the procedure on a relevant
> application in quantum sensing, where we use a quadratic phase on the PMF to implement a
> broadband photon spectrum while maintaining high nonlinearity across the crystal. The designed
> crystal is experimentally tested, and the result matches expectations.
