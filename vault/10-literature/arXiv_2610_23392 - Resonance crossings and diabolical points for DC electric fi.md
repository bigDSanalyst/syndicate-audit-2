# Resonance crossings and diabolical points for DC electric field quantum sensors

## Abstract

> Since the 1990s, experiments on double-well heterostructures have highlighted a key phenomenon:
> applying an external DC electric field induces pronounced wave packet oscillations at specific
> field strengths. \ To gain a deeper insight into this effect, a comprehensive quantitative
> analysis of the properties of quantum resonances near a crossing point is required. \ This
> approach lays the theoretical foundation for devising a quantum sensor capable of rapidly and
> efficiently determining whether the strength of an external DC electric field lies within a
> narrow target range.
