# Strong subadditivity of topological entropy on the torus: Rényi order and non-Abelian structure

## Abstract

> We classify strong subadditivity for the canonical topological Rényi functional at finite
> positive orders on an unpunctured torus in the topological quantum field theory of any finite
> unitary modular tensor category. Universal validity means validity for all pure ground states
> and all regular region pairs with transverse or disjoint boundaries and compatible corner
> rounding. At von Neumann order one, the inequality holds universally. For each fixed order
> strictly between zero and one, universal validity is equivalent to pointedness, meaning that
> every simple anyon type has quantum dimension one. For every nonpointed category and each finite
> positive order other than one, we construct a violating pure state and an allowed pair. For a
> fixed pointed category and fixed order above one, universal validity is equivalent to a same-
> order modular Fourier entropy bound for every pure state. Global homological constraints on
> actual unions and intersections and established weighted modular uncertainty prove the order-one
> theorem. For parallel annuli at fixed flux distribution, the order-one functional is affine in
> their positive count. At other orders, unequal quantum dimensions on the occupied support induce
> strict curvature that suitable overlapping regions convert into negative deficits. Under the
> boundary-charge cutting prescription, the von Neumann theorem extends to arbitrary logical
> density matrices in the same ground space.
