# Quantum Simulation of Non-Markovian Open Quantum Systems via Low-Rank Kernel Compression and Coupling Dilution

## Abstract

> We present an error-controlled framework for the quantum simulation of non-Markovian open-system
> dynamics in Gaussian environments. For a system coupled linearly to a Gaussian environment with
> zero mean, the bath's influence on the reduced dynamics is completely determined by its two-time
> correlation kernel. This allows us to replace the microscopic environment with an equivalent
> bath that has the same kernel. On a finite simulation interval, a rank-$N$ approximation of the
> kernel yields an effective bath of $N$ bosonic modes with time-dependent couplings to the
> system. To encode these modes in qubits, we first truncate each mode to its lowest $d$
> occupation levels. When stronger couplings lead to large occupations, we introduce coupling
> dilution, which replaces each mode with several more weakly coupled copies while preserving the
> bath correlation kernel. This distributes excitations across more modes and trades additional
> modes for smaller local dimensions. We derive explicit reduced-state error bounds and establish
> polynomial circuit width and depth in simulation time and inverse accuracy under stated
> spectral-decay and coupling-regularity assumptions, provided elementary unitaries admit
> polynomial-cost implementations.
