# Emergent Attraction and Competing Orders near the Van Hove Singularity in Rhombohedral $n$-layer Graphene

## Abstract

> Unconventional superconductivity in rhombohedral $n$-layer graphene exhibits a rich phase
> diagram with multiple distinct superconducting regions, whose microscopic origins remain
> unclear. Here, we investigate the competition between charge-density-wave (CDW) and pair-
> density-wave (PDW) instabilities near the inner van Hove singularities (VHSs), using a patch
> renormalization-group approach with the realistic band structures incorporated. We identify two
> key findings. First, we find an effective attractive interaction emerging from purely repulsive
> interactions through RG flow in the presence of $C_3$ symmetry breaking. The resulting
> interaction structure, with two repulsive couplings and one attractive coupling, gives rise to
> competing CDW and PDW instabilities. Second, the competition is controlled by the detuning from
> the VHS. While CDW dominates near the VHS filling and at higher temperatures, PDW becomes the
> leading instability on either side of the VHS and at lower temperatures. This mechanism
> naturally produces two superconducting regions separated by a CDW-dominated regime, providing a
> possible qualitative explanation for the experimentally observed separation between SC1 and SC2.
