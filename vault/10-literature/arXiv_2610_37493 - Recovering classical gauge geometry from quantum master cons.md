# Recovering classical gauge geometry from quantum master constraints

## Abstract

> Exact normalizable solutions of quantum constraints form a symplectic submanifold of the space
> of rays, whereas classical first-class constraints define a surface on which the symplectic form
> is degenerate. We ask how the degenerate directions can be recovered when quantum theory is
> regarded as fundamental and the constraints are imposed through a single positive master
> operator. For a nonnegative master function whose zero set is a Morse--Bott critical manifold,
> the image of the linearized Hamiltonian vector field is the symplectic orthogonal of the zero
> set, and coisotropy is equivalent to this linearization having square zero. The image is fixed
> by the zero set, but the theorem identifies it with the response of the master dynamics, which
> makes it accessible to quantum states. Three realizations follow. The exact quantum physical
> space is the opposite, rotational case of the same dichotomy. For quadratic masters, the bottom
> of the spectrum is $(\hbar/2)\Tr_+L$, so first-class zero sets carry no offset of order $\hbar$;
> within the harmonic approximation this explains the $\hbar$ versus $\hbar^2$ offsets observed in
> tests of the master-constraint programme. Finally, the second time derivative of the limiting
> covariance of a semiclassical packet, which can be determined by bounded probes, has the gauge
> distribution as its image. Two oscillators at fixed total energy give exact master evolution, an
> exact realization of this covariance shear, and the reduced symplectic limit. For the non-
> Abelian SL(2,$\R$) model we compute the coherent and Weyl master symbols, prove essential self-
> adjointness and a finite-time propagation estimate, and derive the spectral bottom $\hbar^2/2$.
