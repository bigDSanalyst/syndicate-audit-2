# Convergence of a finite element Chorin projection method for the stochastic Navier--Stokes equations

## Abstract

> We analyze a fully discrete finite element projection method for the stochastic incompressible
> Navier--Stokes equations with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions and multiplicative
> noise. We show solvability and uniform moment estimates for the intermediate and the corrected
> velocity approximations under the assumption that the initial data is square-integrable and
> divergence free, and that the deterministic forcing term is bounded in $L^{q}(0,T;H^{-1}(D))$
> for a sufficiently large $q$. Under standard stability and approximation assumptions on the
> finite element spaces and a coupling condition on the spatial and temporal discretization
> parameters, we prove convergence of a subsequence to a martingale solution of the stochastic
> Navier--Stokes equations. The analysis combines the complementary spatial and temporal estimates
> of the predicted and the corrected velocity approximations and uses the numerical dissipation to
> identify their common limit. We establish tightness of the piecewise constant temporal
> interpolations of the velocity field approximations using Aldous' criterion combined with the
> uniform discrete moment estimates. The convergence result holds in two and three space
> dimensions as it does not rely on any additional regularity of the limiting martingale solution
> beyond the one naturally available from the energy inequality. Finally, we demonstrate the
> performance of the scheme in numerical experiments in two space dimensions for smooth and vortex
> sheet initial data.
