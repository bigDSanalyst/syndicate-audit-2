# Inducing cooperative dynamics in an inhomogeneous many-emitter ensemble with a pulse sequence

## Abstract

> Driving a many-particle quantum system with an appropriately tuned external field can give rise
> to features in its nonequilibrium dynamics that are absent in the equilibrium counterpart.
> Cooperative dynamics in a dense ensemble of quantum emitters manifested through superradiance, a
> burst in the emission intensity as a function of time, is typically suppressed by spectral
> disparities among emitters in the ensemble. We demonstrate the enhancement of cooperative
> dynamics in a spectrally inhomogeneous ensemble of quantum emitters when it is driven by a
> periodic pulse sequence. Using both a semi-analytical mean-field solution and a digital quantum
> simulation, we examine the coherence of the pulse-driven ensemble and the intensity of the
> emitted radiation as a function of the spectral disparity in the ensemble, as a function of
> time, the number of atoms in the system, and as a function of the inter-pulse delay. We identify
> clear signatures of the enhanced cooperative dynamics for the pulse-driven inhomogeneous
> ensemble when dephasing in the dynamics of the ensemble is suppressed by a sufficient number of
> pulses during the individual emitter lifetime. These results demonstrate a post-fabrication
> tuning knob based on externally applied control fields for the dynamics of the many-emitter
> ensemble.
