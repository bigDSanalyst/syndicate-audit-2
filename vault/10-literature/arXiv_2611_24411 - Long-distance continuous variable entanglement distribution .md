# Long-distance continuous variable entanglement distribution over deployed fibre

## Abstract

> Continuous-variable (CV) entanglement offers deterministic generation and room-temperature
> coherent detection for quantum networks, but distributing it over deployed fibre has been
> hindered by the need to stabilise optical phase, polarisation, and timing at remote receivers.
> Here we demonstrate, to our knowledge, the first distribution of CV entanglement over deployed
> metropolitan fibre using independent coherent receivers. The two modes of a two-mode squeezed
> vacuum state are delivered to remote nodes through 23 km ($\approx 7$ dB loss) and 450 m
> ($\approx 1$ dB loss) deployed fibre links. Each node uses its own free-running laser and
> polarisation-diverse coherent receiver. Co-propagating pilot tones enable digital recovery of
> timing, phase and polarisation, while inter-mode correlations fix the quadrature alignment. We
> certify entanglement with a Simon-Duan value of $1.915\pm 0.013$, below the separability bound
> of 2 and a logarithmic negativity of $0.067\pm0.011$. By replacing optical stabilisation with
> digital reconstruction, our approach offers a practical route towards scalable CV quantum
> networks.
