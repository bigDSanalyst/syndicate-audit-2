# An Excursion into Grassmannian Spectra

## Abstract

> We revisit the classical spectral problem for the Laplace-Beltrami and Laplace-Bochner operators
> on complex Grassmannians. After reviewing the standard approach based on non-commutative
> harmonic analysis, we develop two complementary methods originating from the quantization of the
> corresponding one-dimensional sigma model, both with and without a magnetic monopole field. The
> first is based on $\mathbf{SU}(n)$-equivariant first-order GLSM formulation and gives explicit
> eigenfunctions in terms of projectors and Plücker coordinates. The second uses a spin-chain
> realization of free particles on manifolds and describes the space of square-integrable
> functions, or sections in the magnetic case, through limiting tensor products of long
> rectangular $\mathbf{SU}(n)$ representations. Together, these approaches provide a more explicit
> picture of the spectral geometry of Grassmannians and clarify the relation between harmonics,
> Young diagrams, magnetic bundles, and Laplacian spectra.
