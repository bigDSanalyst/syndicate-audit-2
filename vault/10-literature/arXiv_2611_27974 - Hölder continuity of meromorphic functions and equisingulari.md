# Hölder continuity of meromorphic functions and equisingularity

## Abstract

> For a complex analytic variety and an exponent between zero and one, we study the sheaf of
> meromorphic functions that are locally Hölder continuous with that exponent for the outer
> metric. It sits between the structure sheaves of the seminormalization and of the Lipschitz
> saturation of Pham and Teissier. We prove that these sheaves are coherent and that only finitely
> many of them are distinct, the exponents at which they change being rational numbers governed by
> the Rees valuations of the ideal of a certain diagonal. Each exponent defines a Hölder
> saturation of the variety. We show that a variety whose Hölder saturation is smooth for some
> exponent larger than one third is itself smooth and that one third is sharp. For reduced curve
> germs we compute the Hölder saturations from the characteristic exponents of the branches and
> give explicit thresholds, in terms of classical invariants, at which they become the
> seminormalization, the Lipschitz saturation, or a curve of the same multiplicity. We prove that
> homeomorphic curves are bi-Hölder homeomorphic for some positive exponent, whereas bi-Hölder
> homeomorphisms with exponent close enough to one preserve the multiplicity. We then introduce
> the relative Hölder saturation of a family and prove that it specializes to the saturations of
> the fibers over a dense Zariski-open subset of the base. This leads to two notions of
> equisingularity for families of complex analytic varieties, Hölder equisingularity, defined by a
> bi-Hölder trivialization, and the stronger Hölder equisaturation, defined by the triviality of
> the relative saturation. For families of reduced curve germs we show that they interpolate
> between topological, Whitney and Lipschitz equisingularity, with explicit exponents depending
> only on the invariants of the special fiber.
