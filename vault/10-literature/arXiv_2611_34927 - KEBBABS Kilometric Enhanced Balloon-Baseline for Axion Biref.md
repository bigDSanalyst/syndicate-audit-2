# KEBBABS: Kilometric Enhanced Balloon-Baseline for Axion Birefringence Search

## Abstract

> We propose a broadband search for ultralight axion dark matter through the rotation of laser
> polarization induced by the axion--photon coupling. Two stratospheric balloons flying at
> altitudes of 30--40 km form a free-space optical link. One balloon sends a linearly polarized
> beam to the other, which reflects it back to a polarimeter on the first balloon. The long
> physical baseline provides an alternative to the repeated reflections used in resonant optical
> cavities. We target axion masses in the range $4\times10^{-15}\,\mathrm{eV}\lesssim
> m_a\lesssim10^{-11}\,\mathrm{eV}$. The lower edge corresponds to a frequency of about
> $1\,\mathrm{Hz}$, below which gondola roll about the line of sight can mimic a polarization
> signal. At $m_a=10^{-14}\,\mathrm{eV}$, a low-cost Pathfinder using flight-demonstrated
> technology could probe couplings as small as $g_{aγ}\simeq2.8\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$,
> about a factor of two below the CAST bound. At the same mass, the Feasible today configuration
> could probe couplings just below the astrophysical bounds considered here, while the Futuristic
> configuration could reach $g_{aγ}\simeq6.4\times10^{-15}\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$, roughly two orders
> of magnitude below those bounds.
