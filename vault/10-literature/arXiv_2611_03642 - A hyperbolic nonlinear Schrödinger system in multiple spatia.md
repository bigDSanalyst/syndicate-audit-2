# A hyperbolic nonlinear Schrödinger system in multiple spatial dimensions: structure, convergence, and numerical discretization

## Abstract

> We propose and study a first-order hyperbolic approximation of the nonlinear Schrödinger (NLS)
> equation in $N$ spatial dimensions. This is an extension of the recently-proposed 1D hyperbolic
> NLS model. We study the structure and properties of the hyperbolic approximation and their
> relation to those of the original system. We show that the N-dimensional hyperbolic system
> exactly conserves analogs of the mass, energy, and momentum, and that it possesses stationary
> solutions that are closely related to those of NLS. The hyperbolic system is also shown to admit
> plane-wave solutions that share some stability properties with those of NLS, and that relaxation
> can shrink the focusing low-wavenumber modulational instability band as well as introduce a
> high-wavenumber instability band not present in NLS. We also show that solutions of the
> hyperbolic system converge to solutions of NLS in the relaxation limit. We present an adaptive-
> mesh finite volume discretization of the hyperbolic system and demonstrate its effectiveness in
> handling simulations, including those with geometric obstacles or non-periodic boundary
> conditions. The effectiveness of the numerical scheme is demonstrated through examples including
> a Bose-Einstein condensate, line soliton instability, and superflow past a cylinder.
