# Quantum circuits of elastic wave equations via Schrödingerisation

## Abstract

> We construct explicit quantum circuits for the velocity-stress formulation of elastic wave
> equations in homogeneous media using Schrödingerisation. After rewriting the equations in
> symmetric matrix form(SMF), we use Fourier spectral discretization for source-driven periodic
> problems and staggered-grid finite differences for Dirichlet, Neumann, and impedance boundary
> conditions. The Schrödingerisation method transforms any linear PDEs into a system of
> Schrödinger-type PDEs -with unitary evolution- using the warped phase transformation that maps
> the equations in one higher dimension. For periodic problems, we develop circuits by combining
> controlled Fourier-mode rotations. For physical boundaries, interleaved encoding swaps the
> spatial and field-component registers, enabling a more efficient circuit construction using
> shift operators. We analyze $p$th-order Trotter-Suzuki formulas and derive splitting-error and
> CNOT-gate estimates, under the assumptions of smooth auxiliary initialization and solution-state
> recovery. Comparison with classical partitioned splitting methods identifies a polynomial
> improvement in spatial-resolution dependence for the quantum state-evolution subroutine.
> Noiseless circuit simulations of a forced periodic problem and an impedance problem illustrate
> the constructions and quantify their errors against exact solutions and classical reference
> computations.
