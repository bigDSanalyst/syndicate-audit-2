# Ricci flow of Integrable Sigma Models, Hitchin Systems and Quantum Seiberg-Witten theory

## Abstract

> We construct a family of integrable 2d sigma models through 4d Chern-Simons theory and show that
> their classical point-particle reduction corresponds to a Hitchin system. The integrable Hitchin
> quantisation then provides a natural candidate Hamiltonian for the one-loop reduced dynamics of
> the sigma models, which coincides with the Perelman operator in all cases examined. This allows
> us to study the corresponding spectral problems using 4d $\mathcal{N}=2$ gauge theory and 2d
> conformal field theory methods. For the sausage model, we derive exact quantisation conditions
> and compute exponentially small corrections essential for the agreement with numerical finite-
> volume results.
