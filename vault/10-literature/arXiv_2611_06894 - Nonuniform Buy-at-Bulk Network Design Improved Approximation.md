# Nonuniform Buy-at-Bulk Network Design: Improved Approximation and LP Integrality Gap

## Abstract

> In nonuniform buy-at-bulk network design, we are given an undirected graph and $h$ terminal
> pairs with positive demands, and we must route every demand at minimum total cost when each
> network resource has two independent parameters: a fixed buying price paid once if the resource
> is used, and a per-unit rental cost (or physical length) paid proportionally to the traffic it
> carries. Unlike the uniform-cost or single-sink variants, the general multicommodity problem
> allows unrelated buying and rental metrics across arbitrary source--sink pairs, arbitrary
> demands, and both edge and node resources. We give a deterministic polynomial-time $O(\log^2 h /
> (\log\log h)^2)$-approximation algorithm for undirected nonuniform buy-at-bulk network design.
> The guarantee holds for edge costs, node costs, and their combination, and applies to arbitrary
> positive rational demands with no dependence on the number of vertices or the numerical demand
> ratio. This improves upon the $O(\log^4 h)$ bound of Chekuri, Hajiaghayi, Kortsarz, and
> Salavatipour (2010) for unrestricted instances. Our result also establishes the same $O(\log^2 h
> / (\log\log h)^2)$ upper bound on the integrality gap of the natural affine multicommodity flow
> LP, bounding integral buying and rental costs separately against their fractional counterparts
> and preserving zero rental budgets exactly.
