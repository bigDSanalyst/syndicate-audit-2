# Transfer of Weisfeiler-Leman equivalence under graph operations

## Abstract

> The Weisfeiler-Leman algorithms WL(k) for $k\ge1$ form a hierarchy of increasingly powerful
> tests for graph isomorphism. We study how the equivalence relations they induce on graphs
> transfer through a graph operation $Φ$: for a given $k$, what level of WL-equivalence between
> graphs $H_1$ and $H_2$ guarantees WL(k)-equivalence of graphs $Φ(H_1)$ and $Φ(H_2)$? We call the
> least such level the transfer parameter $τ_Φ(k)$ and determine it exactly for the line graph
> operation $Φ= L$: $τ_L(k)=2k+1$ for $k\ge2$, while $τ_L(1)=1$. We also extend these results to
> the $(r,S)$-line graphs, which generalize the line graph from edges to $r$-cliques by connecting
> two cliques when their intersection size lies in a prescribed set $S$. For these operations we
> prove that $τ_{r,S}(1)=r-1$ for every $S$ and, for certain choices of $S$, that
> $τ_{r,S}(k)=rk+r-1$ for $k\ge r$, and we bound the transfer parameter in the remaining range.
> Our upper bounds on transfer parameters follow from a treewidth analysis of diagrammatic
> expansions of homomorphism counts into $Φ(H)$ as linear combinations of homomorphism counts into
> $H$. The matching lower bounds combine explicit constructions of witness graphs with the
> homomorphism-distinguishing closedness of graphs of bounded treewidth.
