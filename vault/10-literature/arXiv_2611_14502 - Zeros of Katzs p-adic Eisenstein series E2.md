# Zeros of Katz's p-adic Eisenstein series E2

## Abstract

> For every prime $p\geq5$, the zeros of Katz's $p$-adic Eisenstein series $E_2$ on elliptic
> curves with good ordinary reduction have exactly $\lfloor p/12\rfloor+1$ distinct
> $j$-invariants. We prove that all these invariants lie in finite unramified extensions of
> $\mathbf{Q}_p$. Their reductions are given by a classical formula involving the supersingular
> polynomial, and each nonelliptic residue class contains a unique simple zero. For the Legendre
> family, hypergeometric congruences determine the polynomial of the lifted invariants modulo
> every power of $p$. We also study isogenies between the zeros. Each $p$-power isogeny class
> contains at most one zero $j$-invariant, and any two distinct isogenous zero invariants are
> algebraic over $\mathbf{Q}$. At a fixed cyclic degree, the nonelliptic isogenies give points in
> a finite divisor over $\mathbf{Q}$ on the corresponding modular curve. Its degree bounds their
> number and their fields of definition, and excludes several small degrees. The only zeros with
> complex multiplication have $j=0$ or $1728$. Whether a non-CM zero can have an algebraic
> $j$-invariant remains open.
