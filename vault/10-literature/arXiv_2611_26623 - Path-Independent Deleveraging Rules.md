# Path-Independent Deleveraging Rules

## Abstract

> We characterise path-independent auto-deleveraging (ADL) rules as an extended log-exponential
> family. Immunity to account splitting, merging and reopening further restrict the class.
> Homogeneity (essentially) restricts the class to a one-parameter family ordered by both fairness
> and platform risk. Its split-proof members continuously interpolate between proportional
> division and water-filling. Among these rules, decreasing the parameter reduces risk at the cost
> of increasing inequality in how accounts are treated. This suggests selecting the parameter
> through an ex-ante calibration to a risk bound on historical data to choose a maximally fair
> rule subject to that bound. A counterfactual replay of Hyperliquid's ADL episode on 10 October
> 2025 finds that a range of intermediate parameters significantly improves on the observed
> allocation in both burden dispersion and subsequent risk statistics.
