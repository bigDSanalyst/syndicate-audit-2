# Is Neutrino Disappearance Sensitive to T-Violation for Asymmetric Matter Profiles ?

## Abstract

> It is commonly stated in the literature that T violation is zero in neutrino disappearance
> channels. We show this claim is not general: it holds only for symmetric matter profiles or when
> the T/CP-violating phase ($δ$) is 0 or $π$, that is, when the Jarlskog invariant is zero. The
> $ν_μ$ and $ν_τ$, but not the $ν_e$, survival probabilities carry a $\sinδ$-dependent term that
> is non-zero in asymmetric matter and constitutes a genuine T asymmetry. The confusion arises
> from an improper T test that subtracts a probability from itself: a mathematical identity, not a
> physics result. In this paper, we clarify the distinctions between a proper and an improper T
> test as well as the proper and improper CP and CPT tests, for a general, arbitrary matter
> profile. For a toy model consisting of two different layers of constant density, we find that
> the maximum T-violation is obtained when one layer is close to the solar resonance, and the
> other is close to the atmospheric resonance. We address whether the matter asymmetry for DUNE
> and Hyper-Kamiokande experiments is sufficiently large to allow the $\sinδ$ dependence to be
> probed in $ν_μ$ survival probability. We also discuss future experimental scenarios that could
> probe T/CP-violation in $ν_μ$ survival probability.
