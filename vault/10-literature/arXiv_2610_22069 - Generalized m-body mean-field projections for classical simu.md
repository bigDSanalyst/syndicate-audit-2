# Generalized $m$-body mean-field projections for classical simulation of $d$-dimensional Heisenberg-picture dynamics

## Abstract

> Classical simulation of quantum many-body dynamics is challenged by the rapid growth of operator
> complexity, particularly in higher dimensions where tensor-network methods become increasingly
> demanding. We develop state-adapted Pauli-weight propagation based on generalized $m$-body mean-
> field projections, which compress many-body observables into optimal low-body representations in
> a covariance geometry induced by the initial state. Combined with adaptive Krylov-like
> restricted Heisenberg dynamics, this provides an alternative to conventional Hilbert--Schmidt
> Pauli-weight truncation. We derive necessary and conditional sufficient bounds on low-body
> approximation accuracy using standard operator complexity measures and characterize possible
> dynamical failure mechanisms of the state-adapted method. Numerical benchmarks demonstrate
> substantial state-dependent accuracy gains, while identifying regimes of reduced accuracy or
> violations of Pauli spectral bounds. For a three-dimensional system of $80$ interacting spins,
> our method achieves strong body-order convergence, close agreement with independently converging
> tensor-network simulations, and substantially smaller sparse Pauli representations. These
> results establish state-adapted Pauli propagation as a promising approach to classical
> simulation of higher-dimensional coherent many-body dynamics.
