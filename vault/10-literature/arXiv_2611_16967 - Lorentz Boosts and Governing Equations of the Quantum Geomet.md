# Lorentz Boosts and Governing Equations of the Quantum Geometric Tensor in 4D Energy-Momentum Space

## Abstract

> In solids, the quantum geometric tensor (QGT), $Q_{μν}=g_{μν}-\frac{i}{2} Ω_{μν}$, combines the
> quantum metric and Berry curvature. Its imaginary part governs anomalous velocity and quantized
> Hall transport, while its real part determines the quantum weight and geometric contributions to
> flat-band superfluid stiffness and dielectric response. Here, we extend the QGT to four-
> dimensional (4D) energy-momentum space ($ω/c,k$), treating frequency and momentum as independent
> coordinates. Invariance of the real-spacetime plane-wave phase fixes their Lorentz
> transformation in energy-momentum space. Lorentz boosts of static Dirac and Weyl systems then
> generate frequency metrics, quadrupolar frequency-momentum shear metrics, and electric-like
> Berry-Maxwell fields. We identify five Lorentz-invariant contractions and derive the governing
> field equations and flow conservation laws for both the quantum metric and Berry curvature from
> static Weyl geometry and Lorentz covariance. We further connect Gaussian spectral amplitudes of
> the QGT to localized Gaussian events in 4D spacetime. Finally, we discuss experimental
> realizations in spacetime crystals or time-varying media and clarify how this unified quantum
> geometry of a gravitational-like metric and electromagnetic-like gauge fields connects geometric
> self-consistency with physical event dynamics.
