# Measures of maximal entropy for meromorphic maps on compact Kähler manifolds

## Abstract

> Let $f$ be a dominant meromorphic self-map of a compact Kähler manifold. Assume that $f$ has a
> strictly dominant intermediate dynamical degree $λ$ and that the number of isolated $n$-periodic
> points, counted with multiplicity, is at most $λ^n+o(λ^n)$. Then $f$ admits at most one adapted
> invariant probability measure of entropy $\logλ$. If such a measure exists, its natural
> extension is Bernoulli, and the isolated periodic points equidistribute towards it when counted
> with or without multiplicity. We apply this criterion to several classes of maps in complex
> dynamics, resolving longstanding open problems.
