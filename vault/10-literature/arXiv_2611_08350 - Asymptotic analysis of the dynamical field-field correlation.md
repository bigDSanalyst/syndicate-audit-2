# Asymptotic analysis of the dynamical field-field correlation function of the impenetrable Bose gas

## Abstract

> We study the long-time, large-distance asymptotic behaviour of the two-point correlation
> function of fields of the impenetrable Bose gas in equilibrium states characterized by a quasi-
> momentum distribution (or filling fraction). This includes, in particular, the cases of Gibbs
> and generalized Gibbs ensembles. The overall structure of the asymptotic expansion depends on
> the number of Fermi points, those points for which the quasi-momentum distribution takes the
> value $1/2$. The leading asymptotic behaviour is determined by a relaxation rate, a logarithmic
> correction term and an amplitude for which we provide simple and explicit expressions. In the
> case of two Fermi points the first sub-leading correction is calculated as well.
