# Subwavelength confinement of optical and acoustic waves for enhancing Brillouin gain in a planar waveguide structure

## Abstract

> We propose a planar waveguide structure consisting of two layers separated by a gap that
> provides subwavelength confinement of optical and acoustic modes and a large overlap integral
> between them. This, in turn, leads to an increase in Brillouin gain. Using the structure of two
> Si$_3$N$_4$ layers with a subwavelength gap from SiO$_2$ as an example, we demonstrate that a
> hybridized transverse-magnetic (TM) optical mode exhibits strong field confinement in the
> SiO$_2$ gap. Simultaneously, a high-frequency acoustic mode can be confined in the same gap,
> providing a high spatial overlap between the optical and acoustic fields. This provides a high
> opto-acoustic interaction with a Brillouin gain up to $5$ W$^{-1}$m$^{-1}$. The proposed
> structure is compatible with planar photonic integration and offers a promising platform for
> compact, low-threshold Brillouin lasers and coherent phonon generation.
