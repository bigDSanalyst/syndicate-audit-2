# Non-sofic Tarski monsters

## Abstract

> We prove the existence of non-sofic Tarski monsters of every sufficiently large prime exponent
> and of torsion-free non-sofic Tarski monsters. The proof combines a theorem of Sandhu--Thom with
> quotient theorems of Semenov. These groups are minimal non-sofic, since all their proper
> subgroups are cyclic. A theorem of Minasyan--Osin--Witzel gives continuum many pairwise non-
> quasi-isometric torsion-free simple examples. Kulikova's quotient theorem also yields non-simple
> minimal non-sofic groups of exponent $p^m$ for every $m\geq2$ and every sufficiently large prime
> $p$. We then study descending chains of non-sofic subgroups in other constructions. We give
> proper self-embeddings of the binary Leavitt unit group, higher Brin--Thompson groups and the
> hyperbolic group studied by Douglas and Mghirbi. The explicit Kun--Thom wreath products and
> doubles are locally graded. These examples are $ω$-non-sofic, and the locally graded ones
> contain no minimal non-sofic subgroup.
