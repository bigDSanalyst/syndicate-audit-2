# Scaling of entanglement with distance between subsystems: detection of phase transitions in the $t'$-$π$-flux model

## Abstract

> The system-size scaling of bipartite entanglement can be used to detect phase transitions. We
> develop a related approach for strongly correlated electron models where large density matrices
> or large system size scaling are inaccessible. To this end, we demonstrate that phase
> transitions of various kinds are signaled by a sharp signature in the first- or second-order
> logarithmic derivatives of the negativity measuring entanglement between small clusters at
> varying distances. Crucially, we show how to mitigate the effect of quantum Monte Carlo noise at
> large distances when computing the negativity. Our focus is on the $t'$-$π$-flux Hubbard model
> using auxiliary-field quantum Monte Carlo simulations. It has the particular advantage of
> showing three distinct phase transitions in a single model: semimetal to band insulator,
> semimetal to antiferromagnetic Mott insulator, and a magnetic order-disorder transition
> belonging to the Gaussian, Gross-Neveu, and O(3) Wilson-Fisher universality classes,
> respectively.
