# A General Master Equation for Two Accelerated Detectors: Stationary States and Entanglement

## Abstract

> We derive a general Gorini-Kossakowski-Lindblad-Sudarshan master equation for two co-located,
> uniformly accelerated two-level detectors weakly coupled to scalar fields in the Minkowski
> vacuum, retaining coherent and dissipative contributions for arbitrary orientations, field
> masses, and spacetime dimensions. For equal orientations, we determine stationary states and
> their entanglement for two couplings to massless fields in four dimensions: symmetric coupling
> yields acceleration-dependent concurrence, whereas rotating-wave coupling gives acceleration-
> independent concurrence.
