# A Counterexample to the Weak Gibbs and Regularity Conjectures

## Abstract

> We provide a counterexample to the weak Gibbs and regularity conjectures. The example is a spin-
> one system on the square lattice with a translation-invariant nearest-neighbor interaction. At
> high temperatures and high on-site field strengths, it has a unique KMS state that does not have
> the weak Gibbs or regularity properties.
