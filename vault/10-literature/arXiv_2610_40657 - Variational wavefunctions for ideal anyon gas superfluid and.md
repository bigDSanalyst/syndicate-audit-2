# Variational wavefunctions for ideal anyon gas: superfluid and skyrmion Hall crystal

## Abstract

> The ground state of an ideal gas of anyons with statistical angle $θ\in(0,π)$ is a long-standing
> open problem, with renewed interest from the recent observations of fractional Chern insulators.
> In this work we construct variational wavefunctions that build in the exact anyon statistics and
> combine theory-guided composite-boson, composite-fermion and parton wavefunctions with neural-
> network correlations on the sphere. For spinless and SU(2) semions we confirm the paired anyon
> superfluid, well below the best crystal states. At $θ=π/3$ liquids and crystals are closer in
> energy for the spinless case. For $θ=π/3$ anyons with SU(3) flavor, we find two candidates. One
> is a skyrmion Hall crystal, in which the anyons form an integer quantum Hall state in the
> effective magnetic field from the spin texture. The other is a chiral color superconductor of
> six-anyon clusters, the color-flavor-locked pairing descendant of a holon metal with SU(3) color
> gauge symmetry and SU(3) flavor symmetry; its wavefunction is a hyperdeterminant rather than a
> product of parton determinants. Within our variational ansatz the color superconductor has
> better energy than both crystals and the color-flavor locked phases with U(1)$^2$ and Z$_3$
> gauge groups. For twisted MoTe$_2$ this suggests that the reentrant integer quantum anomalous
> Hall phase is the skyrmion Hall crystal of the doped anyons, and that the observed
> superconductivity may be the color superconductor, which can undergo a transition to an $f+if$
> BCS superconductor with a jump of the chiral central charge by 3.
