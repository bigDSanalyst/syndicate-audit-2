# Bargmann-invariant witness of the graviton commutator

## Abstract

> We identify the phase of a Bargmann invariant of two matter-wave interferometers as a quantum
> gravity signature that cannot be reproduced by any branchwise-definite classical gravitational
> field. For matter coupled linearly to linearized gravity, the interferometric coherence
> factorizes exactly into a conservative Newtonian phase, the characteristic functional of the
> graviton state, and a commutator phase. For Wigner-positive graviton states, the first two are
> classically reproducible; therefore, decoherence alone cannot certify that gravity is quantized.
> The Bargmann phase isolates the commutator, which is independent of the graviton state and of
> all Newtonian interactions and is bounded by which-path decoherence. For gravity, this
> radiation-reaction effect is sixty orders of magnitude below experimental reach, but trapped-ion
> analogs can test it at order unity.
