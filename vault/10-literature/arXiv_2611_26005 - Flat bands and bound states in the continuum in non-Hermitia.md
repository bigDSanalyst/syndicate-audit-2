# Flat bands and bound states in the continuum in non-Hermitian pseudospin lattices

## Abstract

> Flat bands support compact localized states (CLSs), whereas bound states in the continuum (BICs)
> remain spatially localized despite having energies embedded in the continuum of extended states.
> Here, we investigate the connection between these phenomena in a class of one-dimensional non-
> Hermitian lattices whose Bloch Hamiltonians take the pseudospin form H(k)=d(k)S. For integer
> pseudospin, the Hamiltonian intrinsically supports a zero-energy flat band and its associated
> compact localized states. When the effective pseudospin field has real transverse components and
> a purely imaginary longitudinal component, increasing non-Hermiticity compresses the dispersive
> bands toward the intrinsic zero-energy flat band, enabling their spectral overlap within a
> finite parameter range. The CLSs thus become embedded in the dispersive continuum and emerge as
> BICs. Adding a momentum-dependent Hermitian component along the gain-loss direction generally
> removes this spectral overlap without destroying the CLSs. We further extend this spectral
> mechanism to integer-pseudospin-n systems. These findings establish a connection between
> intrinsic flat-band localization and BIC formation through non-Hermitian spectral
> reconstruction.
