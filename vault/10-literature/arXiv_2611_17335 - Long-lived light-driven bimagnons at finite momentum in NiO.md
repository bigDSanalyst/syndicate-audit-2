# Long-lived light-driven bimagnons at finite momentum in NiO

## Abstract

> Light can manipulate magnetic order and its excitations on ultrafast timescales, including
> through resonantly driven phonons. In antiferromagnets, light can also create correlated magnon
> pairs, or bimagnons. Unlike single magnons, whose damping has been measured and modeled in
> detail, little is known about the lifetime, interactions and decay of such correlated pairs:
> prior two-magnon measurements show broad linewidths or coherences that dephase within 200 fs.
> Here we pump the phonon-assisted two-magnon absorption of NiO with mid-infrared pulses, far
> below its bandgap, and probe the resulting magnetic dynamics at finite momentum with time-
> resolved resonant inelastic X-ray scattering (RIXS). Upon photoexcitation, the one-magnon
> linewidth and energy are essentially unchanged, indicating that the long-range magnetic order is
> preserved without the photocarrier-induced demagnetization characteristic of above-bandgap
> excitation. A pump-induced two-magnon signal appears on the energy-gain (anti-Stokes) side of
> the spectrum. Its spectral weight peaks 4 ps after the pump, well beyond our 0.5 ps time
> resolution, indicative of a delayed growth likely due to momentum redistribution of the light-
> induced magnon pairs across the Brillouin zone. This two-magnon signal decays with a time
> constant of $28\pm11$ ps, exceeding the previously reported two-magnon dephasing time by more
> than two orders of magnitude. These results establish long-lived light-driven bimagnon
> correlations beyond the one-magnon state, akin to the higher-order correlations measured in
> ultracold gases.
