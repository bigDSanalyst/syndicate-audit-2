# The Disordered Universe 2025: Open Effective Field Theories

## Abstract

> Effective field theories (EFTs) offer a powerful method to unify diverse models under a small
> set of control parameters, allowing systematic expansions around well-established theories.
> While these techniques were traditionally developed for particle physics experiments with clean,
> isolated initial states, realistic macroscopic environments are often noisy and dissipative.
> Recognising the limitations of traditional EFTs has recently sparked intense progress at the
> interface of high-energy physics and condensed matter. These developments motivate a new
> approach to gravitation and cosmology, one that models the gravitational sector as evolving in
> the presence of an unobservable medium. Open Effective Field Theories provide a systematic,
> controllable framework for this endeavour. This chapter presents this versatile framework and
> illustrates how the Schwinger-Keldysh formalism captures the influence of unknown environments,
> manifesting as dissipation and noise, on observational probes. These notes are based on a series
> of lectures held during the La Ricotta Summer School "The Disordered Universe 2025" from 21 July
> to 1 August 2025.
