# Holographic two-point functions across a PT symmetry-breaking transition

## Abstract

> In parity-time (PT)-symmetric non-Hermitian systems, an exceptional point, where eigenvalues and
> eigenvectors coalesce, typically separates a real spectrum from complex-conjugate eigenvalues.
> In logarithmic conformal field theories (LCFTs), such an exceptional point appears in the
> spectrum of scaling dimensions. Holographic models of LCFTs, such as the dipole model, are built
> exactly at this point. Here, we deform the holographic dipole model so that its two coalescing
> scaling dimensions split into either two real ones or a complex-conjugate pair. We obtain the
> renormalized two-point functions in closed form. They change continuously from a linear
> combination of two power laws, through the logarithmic form of the LCFT at the exceptional
> point, to log-periodic oscillations. For any pair of scalar primary operators with real mixing
> under dilatations, the conformal Ward identities fix this form up to two normalization
> constants, which the holographic dictionary determines. In PT-symmetric theories with such a
> dilatation operator, real and complex-conjugate scaling dimensions correspond to the PT-unbroken
> and PT-broken phases. In the BTZ black hole at finite temperature, the difference between the
> quasinormal frequencies associated with the two scaling dimensions changes from purely imaginary
> to purely real across the real-to-complex spectral transition. Correspondingly, the linear
> response changes from overdamped to underdamped, with critical damping at the exceptional point.
