# Predictive electronic feedback control for narrow-linewidth lasers

## Abstract

> Narrow-linewidth lasers are essential for various key atom-laser interaction processes and
> optical setups in quantum coherent control, quantum precision measurement, and quantum
> information science. The Pound-Drever-Hall (PDH) technique is the standard method for the
> frequency-stabilization of narrow-linewidth lasers, but the inherent time delay in its feedback
> loop inevitably induces excessive phase noise, commonly known as servo bumps. Previous solutions
> primarily rely on additional optical components outside the electronic feedback control loop.
> Here, we propose and implement a generic method based on the Smith predictor to address the
> time-delay issue entirely within the electronic part of the PDH feedback control loop. We
> systematically demonstrate its capability to suppress phase noise and reduce servo bumps by
> adding only a single Field-Programmable Gate Array (FPGA) controller to the loop. This class of
> methods is demonstrated to be conceptually concise with minimal hardware overhead, effective in
> practice, and holds potential for a wide variety of future applications.
