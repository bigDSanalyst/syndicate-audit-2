# A hierarchy of exact lower bounds on the Laughlin spectral gap

## Abstract

> We develop a bootstrap approach to deriving lower bounds on the spectral gap of the first
> Haldane pseudopotential Hamiltonian on the sphere with $N_Φ$ flux quanta for arbitrary $N_Φ$,
> including in the thermodynamic limit $N_Φ\to \infty$. Our approach can in principle be
> generalized to pseudopotentials describing other model wavefunctions such as the Moore-Read
> state or Read-Rezayi sequence. Our approach is based on Knabe's observation that if $H$ is a
> Hamiltonian with ground state energy $0$ then the spectral gap is at least $ε> 0$ if and only if
> $H^2 \succeq εH$. To show this inequality, we split $H^2$ into two parts, one that can be
> analytically bounded and another that can be bounded using a finite semidefinite program (SDP).
> Crucially, the size of this program is determined only by cutoff parameters, and the program
> depends on $N_Φ$ only parametrically, allowing us to obtain numerically exact (up to solver
> precision) lower bounds on the gap. In order to turn these numerical bounds into mathematically
> rigorous bounds, we introduce an algorithm that can produce nearly-optimal exact feasible points
> for large rational SDPs. We additionally provide an algorithm that establishes the rank
> stability of the semidefinite program in the limit $N_Φ\to \infty$ which is needed to certify
> the bound in the thermodynamic limit. Beyond establishing the Laughlin gap and revealing a
> potential method for deriving spectral gap lower bounds for other pseudopotentials, our work
> provides a general method for turning numerical semidefinite programming bounds into rigorous
> mathematical proofs, which may have wide applicability.
