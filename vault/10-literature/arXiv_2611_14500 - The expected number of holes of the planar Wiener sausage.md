# The expected number of holes of the planar Wiener sausage

## Abstract

> ```text Let $β_1^T(r)$ be the number of holes, that is, the first Betti number, of the Wiener
> sausage of radius $r$ of a planar Brownian motion run up to time $T$. We prove that
> $\mathbb{E}[β_1^T(r)]$ is of order $T/(r^2(\log T)^2)$ as $T\to\infty$, for each fixed $r>0$,
> which completes the lower bound obtained in a companion paper from a law for the inradii of the
> cavities of the path. We also identify which parts of the complement of the path contribute at
> leading order. At unit time and radius $\varepsilon$, the holes lying in the unbounded component
> of the complement have expected number $O(\varepsilon^{-2+θ_0})$ for an absolute $θ_0>0$,
> whereas the additional holes created when a cavity fragments under erosion have expected number
> of exact order $\varepsilon^{-2}(\log(1/\varepsilon))^{-2}$. In particular the one-pocket
> constant of the lower bound is strictly improved. The upper bound is obtained by pricing the
> contact configurations that pin near-critical pockets along the two independent arms of the path
> from a deterministic time, each arm contributing one factor $1/\log(1/\varepsilon)$. The outer
> estimate combines a stratified form of this first-moment bound with a packing argument near the
> frontier of the path, and the splitting lower bound forces a dumbbell-shaped cavity by a local
> construction followed by two logarithmic avoidance probabilities. The upper bound and the two
> structural estimates use no limit theorem for the shapes of cavities; the shape law enters only
> through the lower bound of the companion paper. ```
