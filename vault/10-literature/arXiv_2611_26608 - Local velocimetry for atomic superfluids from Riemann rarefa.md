# Local velocimetry for atomic superfluids: from Riemann rarefaction waves to quantized vortices

## Abstract

> We demonstrate local velocimetry for atomic superfluids in two dimensions (2D). Using matter-
> wave interferometry based on Raman--Bragg beam splitters, we map the local gradient of the phase
> of the superfluid's wavefunction onto displacement of the interference fringes and reconstruct
> the 2D velocity field. We illustrate the capabilities of this technique by applying it to two
> canonical problems. First, we directly observe the self-similar evolution of the velocity field
> in 1D Riemann rarefaction flows. Second, we map out the full 2D velocity field around quantized
> vortices, explicitly demonstrating the quantization of velocity circulation.
