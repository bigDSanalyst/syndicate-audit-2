# Complex chaotic Boltzmann machines as deterministic representations of quantum states

## Abstract

> Neural networks and tensor networks have opened new ways to explore quantum systems by
> parameterizing families of wave functions through the structure and parameters of a network.
> Here we extend this perspective to a dynamical representation: we introduce a complex chaotic
> Boltzmann machine that assigns a deterministic system to a pure quantum state. We extend
> Suzuki's construction to incorporate both Born probabilities and relative phases. The
> probabilities set the trajectory velocities in a hypercube, while the phases define a complex
> field that lifts its Poincare sections. The evolution is determined by the network parameters
> and initial conditions, without random choices during flights or configuration changes. Our
> analysis of two qubits and study of three-qubit families show how relations between
> probabilities organize regular curves, islands, and unstable regions, and how phases distinguish
> states that share the same Born dynamics. This architecture turns a wave-function
> parameterization into a geometry of trajectories, providing a tool to explore probabilities,
> phases, and symmetries together across families of states.
