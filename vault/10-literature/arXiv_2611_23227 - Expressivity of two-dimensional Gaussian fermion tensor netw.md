# Expressivity of two-dimensional Gaussian fermion tensor network states

## Abstract

> Tensor network states (TNS) provide a family of efficiently compressed approximations to
> physically relevant many-body quantum states. An important quantity that characterizes the
> computational resources required by a TNS is its bond dimension. While in one dimension an area
> law for the Renyi-0 entropy guarantees an exact matrix product state representation with a bond
> dimension independent of the system size, an analogous statement does not hold for general
> states in two dimensions (2D). This leaves open the question of how the expressivity of 2D TNS
> is related to the entanglement properties of the states they represent. In this work, we focus
> on two families of 2D translation-invariant Gaussian fermionic states: single-band BCS
> superconductors and half-filled two-band insulators (including semimetals). For each family, we
> prove that an area law for the Renyi-0 entropy is equivalent to having an exact Gaussian
> fermionic TNS representation with a finite bond dimension determined by the Renyi-0 entropy. We
> also establish equivalent characterizations in terms of compactly supported Wannier-type
> orbitals and the momentum-space wavefunction, relating the spatial extent of these orbitals and
> the structure of the wavefunction to the required bond dimension.
