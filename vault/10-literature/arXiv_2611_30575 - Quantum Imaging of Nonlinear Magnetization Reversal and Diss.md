# Quantum Imaging of Nonlinear Magnetization Reversal and Dissipative Domain Dynamics

## Abstract

> To investigate the rich nonlinear dynamics of magnetic materials, we introduce a stroboscopic
> quantum diamond microscopy technique to probe the dynamic AC response of a Pt/Co/Pt thin film
> using nitrogen-vacancy centres in diamond. By overcoming the limited dynamic range of
> conventional optically detected magnetic resonance based imaging, our approach enables the
> observation of magnetization reversal under various large-amplitude sinusoidal and square wave
> excitations. The observed phase delay between the applied field and the sample response reveals
> information about the energy dissipated within the material. The temporal evolution reveals
> magnetization reversal through domain nucleation and growth also demonstrating isolated bubble
> domains associated with the defects. Furthermore, the detection of higher order harmonics
> provides access to the nonlinear AC susceptibility. These results establish stroboscopic quantum
> diamond microscope as a promising tool for spatially and temporally resolving nonlinear
> magnetization dynamics, with potential applications extending beyond ferromagnetic systems to
> complex magnetic crystals, magnetic phase transitions and superconductor vortex dynamics.
