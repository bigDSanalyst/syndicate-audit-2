# Stable charged energy and phase transitions in an anisotropic Dicke quantum battery with Kerr nonlinearity

## Abstract

> Phase transitions (PTs) in quantum many-body systems offer a powerful route to manipulate the
> performance of quantum batteries (QBs). Here, we investigate an anisotropic Dicke QB with Kerr
> nonlinearity, focusing on the role of PTs in the charging process of the QB system. By analyzing
> how the Kerr nonlinearity reshapes the cavity level-spacing structure, we find that multiple
> near-degeneracies emerge at specific nonlinearity strengths. Away from the region of multiple
> near-degeneracies, the stable stored energy is governed primarily by the ergodic-nonergodic
> transition (ENET), whereas within this region it is controlled by first- and second-order
> dissipative phase transitions (DPTs). We employ Liouvillian spectral theory to quantify the
> critical slowing down associated with the DPTs, and use the Wigner function to characterize the
> properties of the cavity field in the vicinity of criticality. We optimize the charging process
> via reinforcement learning (RL) and reveal the physical mechanism underlying performance
> enhancement from the perspective of ergodic and nonergodic phase dynamics. Our results offer a
> feasible pathway for manipulating and optimizing quantum battery performance based on intrinsic
> PT behaviors.
