# OpenTac: An Open Ecosystem for Customizable, Scalable, and Transferable 3D Tactile Sensing

## Abstract

> We present OpenTac, a novel sensor and a full-stack ecosystem for tactile skins that are highly
> customizable, scalable, and readily deployed in downstream robot control and learning
> applications. Our core contribution is twofold. First, we develop a scalable resistive tactile
> skin with a structured deformable overlay that converts tangential loading into spatially
> varying pressure. Differential responses across neighboring "taxels" support decoding of local
> shear vectors, and the common-mode response encodes normal force. Second, we provide a highly
> customizable sensor design tool that is able to automatically generate fabrication files for
> these tactile skins from 2D outlines and developable 3D surfaces, including from abstract
> descriptions such as text (e.g., "design a sensor for a size 9 shoe insole"). We develop an
> ecosystem around the OpenTac sensor, including the CAD design tool, a sensor simulator, cross-
> sensor transfer, and fast/adaptive data acquisition. We demonstrate the broad applicability of
> the OpenTac-CAD sensor design tool by designing and fabricating more than 30 sensors across a
> variety of robot embodiments. We characterize the three-dimensional tactile fields produced by
> OpenTac with a commercial, high-precision force/torque sensor. We demonstrate the broad
> applicability of 3D tactile sensing across contact-rich feedback control and manipulation policy
> learning on four real-world robot embodiments. Additionally, adaptive acquisition enables
> kilohertz-rate partial readouts, exceeding full-array raster scanning on the same sensor by
> 19.2X at 16 sampled taxels per update for a 1024-taxel skin. We provide all hardware, software,
> and result videos are at opentac.github.io
