# Low-field dephasing time of an optically polarized NV$^-$ spin bath beyond the P1 limit in diamond: a cluster-correlation-expansion and exact spin-bath study

## Abstract

> Dense ensembles of nitrogen-vacancy (NV$^-$) centres in nitrogen-rich diamond dephase on the
> electron spins of the substitutional nitrogen (P1) centres. If the paramagnetic nitrogen is
> instead converted into NV$^-$ centres, the laser that initializes the sensor also pumps the bath
> into $m_s$ = 0, a state with no moment along its own axis. Using cluster-correlation-expansion
> (CCE) calculations, combined with an exact solver for probe-bath exchange and with no adjustable
> parameter, this paper asks whether such an optically polarized NV$^-$ bath dephases a sensor
> more slowly than a P1 bath of the same density. The answer is set by the field. In a field along
> [111], three quarters of the bath centres lie off the field axis, and the moment of their $m_s$
> = 0 state along the field grows from 0.04 at 25 G to 0.46 at 300 G and 0.81 at 800 G. At 25 G
> the polarized bath is therefore nearly silent: at $10^{17}$ cm$^{-3}$ the dephasing time $T_2^*$
> of a separately addressed $^{15}$NV$^-$ probe is 112 us, six times the 18.1 us of a P1 bath, and
> still 2.4 times at 90 % polarization; an ensemble measurement gains a factor of two. At 300 G
> the advantage over P1 shrinks to 1.4, and at 800 G the polarized bath dephases faster than P1.
> The field dependence of every bath, P1 included, follows the mean moment of its centres along
> the field. For the Hahn echo, exchange with on-axis bath centres and bath relaxation ($T_1$ = 6
> ms) limit the gain to 1.2 times the P1 value at 25 G. Optical polarization of an NV$^-$ bath at
> low field therefore offers dephasing times beyond the P1 limit of nitrogen-rich diamond.
