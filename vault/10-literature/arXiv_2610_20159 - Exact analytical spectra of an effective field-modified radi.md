# Exact analytical spectra of an effective field-modified radial Schrödinger equation by the Nikiforov--Uvarov method

## Abstract

> We revisit the effective radial Schrödinger equation for a charged spinless particle in
> collinear static electric and magnetic fields that was formulated in our earlier work within the
> tridiagonal representation approach (TRA). In that model the angular factor of the electric
> coupling is replaced by the orientation parameter $C=\cosθ$, the linear Zeeman term is diagonal
> in the azimuthal quantum number, and the effective field-modified central potential takes the
> form $V(r)=\Vt(r)+qECr$. Here we show that the same equation can be solved exactly without the
> TRA, by the Nikiforov--Uvarov (NU) method, once the potential component $\Vt(r)$ is prescribed.
> Three examples are worked out in closed form: a Kratzer-type component, which yields a linear-
> plus-Coulomb-plus-inverse-square central potential; a pseudoharmonic-type component, which
> yields a linear-plus-harmonic-plus-inverse-square potential; and a Hulthén-type component with a
> repulsive core (Manning--Rosen form), which yields a screened confining potential with a finite
> number of $s$-wave bound states. For each case we obtain the energy spectrum, the normalized
> eigenfunctions in terms of Laguerre or Jacobi polynomials, the location of the potential
> minimum, and closed-form radial moments. The spectra are independent of the orientation $θ$ and
> of the electric field, in agreement with the structure found by the TRA, while the field enters
> through the shape of $V(r)$, the radial moments, and the expectation value $\langle V\rangle_k$.
> All analytical results are confirmed by an independent finite-difference diagonalization.
