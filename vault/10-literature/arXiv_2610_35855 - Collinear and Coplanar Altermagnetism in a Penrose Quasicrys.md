# Collinear and Coplanar Altermagnetism in a Penrose Quasicrystal

## Abstract

> We generalize the previous group-theory based classification of collinear magnetism in the
> quasicrystal (QC) to general magnetic states. Taking the Penrose lattice as an example, we find
> that all magnetic states belonging to 2D irreducible representations (IRRPs) of the $D_5$ point
> group, including collinear states and coplanar ones, correspond to altermagnetism (AM). Unlike
> conventional collinear AM wherein zero magnetism is protected by, e.g. rotation or mirror
> reflection, which globally flip the spin in the magnetic pattern, the zero magnetism in these
> 2D-IRRP states is instead protected by a special group-theory-orthogonality theorem that basis
> functions belonging to different IRRPs are always mutually orthogonal. Taking the single-orbital
> Penrose-Hubbard as an example, our linear-response theory based calculations show that the
> largest portion of the parameter space is occupied by these 2D-IRRP states, and our further
> Ginzburg-Landau theory based analysis reveals that the polarization directions of the two basis
> magnetic states in the 2D IRRPs can be either parallel or perpendicular, leading to collinear or
> coplanar magnetic states. The special group-theory-orthogonality compensation mechanism and the
> absence of the PT symmetry finally leads to AM states, which are to be verified by the spin
> split in the spin-dependent angle-resolved-photoemission-spectrum and the spin conductance.
> These intriguing collinear and coplanar AM can also emerge in other QCs.
