# No Polynomial-Time Algorithm For Tarski Fixed Points Unless Cryptographic Assumptions Fail

## Abstract

> We study the time complexity of finding a Tarski fixed point. We provide a subexponential-time
> reduction from the Unique End of Potential Line (UEOPL) problem to the problem of finding a
> Tarski fixed point. Using a range of known cryptographic lower bounds for UEOPL, our reduction
> implies a range of conditional lower bounds on the running time of any algorithm that finds a
> Tarski fixed point. The strongest of these give quasi-polynomial-time lower bounds for the
> problem. We also obtain a similar quasi-polynomial-time lower bound for Tarski based instead on
> a weak exponential-time type hypothesis for UEOPL. These running time lower bounds match the
> recently discovered quasi-polynomial query lower bounds for the problem.
