# Enhanced-sensitivity optical sensing of magnetic fields using postselected amplification and continuous-wave power recycling

## Abstract

> Given the importance of precision weak magnetic field measurement, and noting that postselected
> amplification has not yet been well applied in this area, in this work we propose to combine the
> technique of postselected amplification with continuous-wave power recycling for enhanced-
> sensitivity optical sensing of weak magnetic fields. Based on rigorous derivations, we obtain
> compact expressions for the total postselection probability and the signal-to-noise ratio. We
> also analyze the effects originating from various non-ideal factors. In contrast to pulse
> recycling, continuous-wave recycling has lower control requirements and is more experimentally
> feasible.
