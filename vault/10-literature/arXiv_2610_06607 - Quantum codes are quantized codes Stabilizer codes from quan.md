# Quantum codes are quantized codes: Stabilizer codes from quantization over finite fields

## Abstract

> We present a discrete analogue of geometric quantization over finite fields which, when applied
> to certain classical error-correcting codes, yields quantum error-correcting codes. This
> provides a physical interpretation for a previously known quantum-classical correspondence in
> coding theory.
