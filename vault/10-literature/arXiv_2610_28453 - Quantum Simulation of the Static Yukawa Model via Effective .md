# Quantum Simulation of the Static Yukawa Model via Effective Particles on a Trapped-Ion Quantum Simulator

## Abstract

> Trapped-ion, neutral-atom, and circuit QED quantum computers naturally enable coupled qubit-
> boson simulations. Boson-fermion interactions arise in quantum field theory, where
> renormalization is required to obtain finite results. We study one of the simplest examples of a
> coupled fermion-boson system, the static Yukawa model, on the QSCOUT trapped-ion system. We use
> similarity renormalization to express the same physics in terms of effective particles described
> by different values of the renormalization parameter $s$. This enables us to map data taken in
> the window of bosonic occupancy that is experimentally accessible to larger occupancies of
> distinct effective particles. We use this to study strongly coupled, high-occupancy regimes
> despite limited readout ranges. Using a trapped-ion qubit-boson device on Sandia National
> Laboratories' QSCOUT platform, we demonstrate the time evolution of the static Yukawa model.
