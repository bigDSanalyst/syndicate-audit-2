# Hidden parabolic subgroups of $\mathrm{GL}_n$ over finite chain rings

## Abstract

> We give a bounded-error quantum polynomial-time algorithm for finding hidden parabolic subgroups
> of $\mathrm{GL}_n(R)$ over any effectively presented finite chain ring $R$. We call auxiliary
> group elements or subgroups used to extract information through oracle queries \emph{probes}.
> For chain length at least two, elementary abelian socle probes first recover the complete flag
> modulo the maximal ideal. When the residue field has size $q\ge3$, we reconstruct the flag
> recursively, lifting rank-one terminal members one digit at a time. The intermediate digits
> reduce to abelian hidden subgroup problems, while the final digit is a finite-field multiple-
> shift problem solved using learning from subset and Fourier sampling. For $q=2$, where distinct
> flags can have the same stabilizer, we instead lift the conjugate parabolic subgroup through the
> congruence filtration. A final linear system determines it modulo normalizer directions. The
> algorithm outputs generators with probability at least $1-ε$ in time
> $\mathrm{poly}(n,\log|R|,\log(1/ε))$. Consequences include
> $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})$ for every $N\ge2$ and, with an explicit maximal-order
> presentation, $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal{O_K}/N\mathcal{O_K})$ for number fields $K$.
