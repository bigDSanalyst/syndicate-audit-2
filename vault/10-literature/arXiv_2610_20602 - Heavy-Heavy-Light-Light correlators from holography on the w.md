# Heavy-Heavy-Light-Light correlators from holography on the worldsheet

## Abstract

> We study correlation functions of light local operators and defect changing operators on the
> conformal line set by the 1/2 BPS Wilson line of $\mathcal N=4$ super Yang-Mills. We consider
> the defect changing operators associated with geometrical and internal cusp angles, which at
> strong coupling have a large scaling dimension and back-react on the dual $AdS_2$ world-sheet.
> We set up a holographic computation of correlation functions, in which the light operators are
> described by fluctuations around the cusped classical string solution, so that correlators of
> two defect changing operators and an arbitrary number of light protected operators are obtained
> from the on-shell action of these fluctuations. We implement this prescription in detail for the
> case of certain 4-point functions, with two light and two defect changing operators. Finally,
> since for small cusp angles the defect changing operators become light, we manage to derive
> relations satisfied by integrated correlators of light operators, valid at any coupling. We
> verify these relations at strong coupling for both internal and geometrical cusps.
