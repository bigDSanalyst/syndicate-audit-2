# Prospects and limitations of adaptive variational quantum algorithms for lattice field theories: a case study in 2+1 dimensions

## Abstract

> The variational quantum algorithm ADAPT-VQE has previously been used to prepare the vacuum of
> lattice gauge theories in $1+1D$. In this paper, we study whether it extends to higher
> dimensions. We identify two general limitations of ADAPT-VQE. First, if the qubit Hamiltonian
> and the initial state are real, i.e., invariant under qubit-level time-reversal, all real-valued
> pool operators have vanishing gradients and are never selected. Second, the algorithm can
> converge to excited eigenstates. We illustrate both limitations, also providing examples from
> literature, and present a first application of ADAPT-VQE to a lattice field theory in $2+1D$,
> namely free Wilson fermions, where both obstacles occur. Adding charge conjugation symmetry to
> the operator pool improves the results, and combining different pools with suitable initial
> states recovers the ground and first excited states over the full mass range. These findings
> give practical guidelines for applying ADAPT-VQE to lattice field theories beyond $1+1D$.
