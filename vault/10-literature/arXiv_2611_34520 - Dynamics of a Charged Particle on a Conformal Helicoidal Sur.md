# Dynamics of a Charged Particle on a Conformal Helicoidal Surface

## Abstract

> We study the classical and quantum motion of a charged particle on a conformal helicoidal
> surface embedded in four-dimensional Euclidean space. A uniform ambient magnetic field pulls
> back to a nonuniform surface field. The cyclic longitudinal coordinate permits an exact
> transverse reduction. The ordinary helicoid in the same axial field shares the effective
> potential and the turning points, while the conformal metric increases the physical period at
> matched energy and longitudinal momentum. We classify the single- and double-well regimes and
> obtain the exact transverse periods in the two double-well sectors. The periods diverge
> logarithmically at the separatrix, with twice the leading coefficient above the barrier as below
> it. The zero-field limit allows transverse escape, and the weak-twist limit is locally free but
> nonuniform across the surface. Intrinsic quantization gives an exact weighted transverse
> eigenvalue problem. Fixed-momentum eigenvalues test the harmonic approximation in a central well
> and resolve the tunneling splittings in a double well.
