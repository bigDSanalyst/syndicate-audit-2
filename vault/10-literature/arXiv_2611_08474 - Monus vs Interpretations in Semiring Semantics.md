# Monus vs. Interpretations in Semiring Semantics

## Abstract

> Interpretations have been widely used in the study of provenance of queries on semiring-
> annotated databases. Here, we consider two different interpretation-based semantics for first-
> order formulas in negation normal form, namely, the canonical-interpretation semantics and the
> compatible-interpretation semantics. We show that the resulting query languages cannot express
> the monus operation on semiring-annotated relations, when the annotations come from the bag
> semiring or the tropical semiring or the fuzzy semiring. Consequently, the semantics of SQL
> cannot be captured using the interpretation-based semantics considered here.
