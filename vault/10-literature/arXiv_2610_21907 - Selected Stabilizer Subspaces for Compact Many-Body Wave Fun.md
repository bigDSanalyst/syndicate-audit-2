# Selected Stabilizer Subspaces for Compact Many-Body Wave Functions

## Abstract

> Selected configuration interaction (SCI) obtains accurate many-body solutions by variationally
> diagonalising the Hamiltonian in a sparse, iteratively selected set of configurations, but can
> lose compactness when the target state has broad configurational support. We introduce selected
> stabilizer interaction (SSI), a fully classical, SCI-inspired method that instead iteratively
> constructs a nonorthogonal subspace spanned by a systematically enlargeable set of selected
> stabilizer states. A single stabilizer, generated by Clifford operations, can encode a
> structured superposition of (possibly exponentially) many configurations, shifting the relevant
> compression from configurational to stabilizer sparsity. We benchmark ground-state energies,
> fidelities, superposition degrees and required expansion lengths against exact diagonalization
> and equivalent SCI schemes for the transverse-field Ising, XXZ Heisenberg, and Hubbard models,
> finding particularly strong compression from the stabilizer representation in the spin models,
> and increasing advantage with system size for these systems. The results establish a proof of
> principle for heuristic selected stabilizer diagonalization, while also identifying particular
> challenges in symmetry handling and effective exploration of the stabilizer space.
