# Judd-Ofelt analysis and coherent spectroscopy of europium-based molecular complexes for quantum information applications

## Abstract

> Rare-earth-ion based molecular crystals are emerging as a promising platform for quantum
> information applications, combining long optical coherence times with the chemical tunability of
> coordination chemistry. Here we present a comprehensive optical characterisation of twelve
> Eu$^{3+}$-based molecular complexes, with a focus on the coherent $^{5}\text{D}_{0}
> \longrightarrow \ ^{7}\text{F}_{0}$ transition at $\sim$580 nm. Judd-Ofelt analyses are
> performed across all complexes, providing insights into ligand-field environments, local point-
> group symmetries, and the resulting branching ratios of the coherent transition. We find that
> lower point-group symmetry (C$_1$, C$_2$) yields higher branching ratios of the
> $^{5}\text{D}_{0} \longrightarrow \ ^{7}\text{F}_{0}$ transition, exceeding 1\% in several
> complexes. Optical inhomogeneous linewidths measured at 4.2 K reveal narrow linewidths for some
> complexes of below ten GHz, indicative of a highly ordered crystalline environment. Optical
> coherence measurements of one of the complexes, 5-Eu, yields an optical coherence time of
> $T_{\text{2}}^{\text{opt}} = 2.3~μ\mathrm{s}$ at 4.2 K, comparable to the best-performing
> molecular systems reported to date. Systematic characterisation and quantitative comparison will
> be key to find structure-property relations guiding rational design of optimised complexes for
> quantum information applications.
