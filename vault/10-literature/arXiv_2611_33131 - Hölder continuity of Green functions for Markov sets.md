# Hölder continuity of Green functions for Markov sets

## Abstract

> Let $K$ be a nonempty compact subset of $\mathbb C^N$. We prove that its Green function (and
> consequently, the Siciak extremal function) is Hölder continuous if and only if the first
> derivatives of polynomials satisfy Markov's inequality on $K$. The implication from Markov's
> inequality to Hölder continuity of the associated Green function settles a longstanding open
> problem at the intersection of approximation theory and pluripotential theory. A Markov bound
> $An^m$ in degree $n$ gives every Hölder exponent below $1/m$. In one complex variable it gives
> the exponent $1/m$ itself. The proof compares the extremal functions of two neighborhoods of
> $K$. For $N>1$ we use Hörmander's theorem on holomorphic extension from a point. For $N=1$ the
> comparison follows from the maximum principle.
