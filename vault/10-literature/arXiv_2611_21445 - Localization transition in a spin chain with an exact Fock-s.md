# Localization transition in a spin chain with an exact Fock-space tree

## Abstract

> Many-body localization is often pictured as Anderson localization on the graph of many-body
> configurations. Strongly correlated disorder and a dense net of loops, however, make this graph
> hard to analyze. We introduce a local disordered spin-1 chain in which a single domain wall
> explores an exact binary tree of configurations. Random fields create strongly correlated
> disorder on this tree, as in generic interacting systems, while the absence of loops keeps the
> problem tractable. We rigorously bound the spreading of the domain wall at strong disorder, and
> efficient numerics on exponentially large trees indicate a localization transition at finite
> disorder strength.
