# Disorder-Induced Logarithmic Spreading and Localization of Quantum Fluctuations

## Abstract

> Disorder can localize quantum particles, even in the presence of interactions, but its influence
> on quantum fluctuations beyond mean--field dynamics remains poorly understood. Here, we
> investigate the propagation of quantum fluctuations in weakly interacting disordered bosonic
> systems by comparing the Bose--Hubbard model with the Gross--Pitaevskii equation. We find that,
> in contrast to ballistic spreading in the disorder--free system, quantum fluctuations exhibit
> logarithmic spreading at intermediate times under both random and quasiperiodic disorder, before
> eventually saturating. The spreading rate decreases algebraically with increasing disorder
> strength, and this behavior persists across different particle numbers and extends to two
> dimensions. Using a time--dependent Bogoliubov--de Gennes framework, we identify the localized
> mean--field wavefunction as a time--dependent pairing background that generates quantum
> fluctuations. The exponential suppression of fluctuation--generation rates with distance
> provides a physical mechanism for the logarithmically advancing fluctuation front. Our results
> reveal a distinct regime of disorder--controlled quantum--fluctuation dynamics and establish
> disorder as a means of controlling their spreading.
