# Magic Can Superactivate Quantum Capacity

## Abstract

> Environmental magic can enable two channels with zero quantum capacity to transmit quantum
> information jointly. We establish this superactivation within a family of discrete quantum
> convolution channels with fixed Clifford interactions. For the couplings considered, stabilizer-
> mixture environments induce entanglement-breaking channels. We construct a mixed magic
> environment whose induced channel has positive private capacity and a Choi state with positive
> partial transpose, hence zero quantum capacity. A half-erasure auxiliary activates this channel
> through the Smith--Yard mechanism. Finite-block encoding and decoding over a self-complementary
> convolution auxiliary then realize a higher-dimensional erasure channel that still permits
> activation. We also bound the activated capacity by the modified relative entropy of magic of
> the main environment. Replacing either environment of the convolution pair by any stabilizer
> mixture makes the joint quantum capacity vanish, identifying environmental magic as a necessary
> resource on both sides for this realization.
