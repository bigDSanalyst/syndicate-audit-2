# Number-correlated photon entanglement with a quantum memory interferometer

## Abstract

> Strong photon-photon interactions are central to quantum optical technologies yet remain
> challenging to realise at the single-photon level. Here, we show how embedding a quantum memory
> within a Mach-Zehnder interferometer enables a coherent transformation that converts single-mode
> photon states into number-correlated entangled states. Using an off-resonant ladder-type atomic
> interaction, storage and retrieval within a quantum memory maps a single-photon excitation onto
> a pair of photons across distinct optical modes via a driving control field. Embedding this
> within an interferometer deterministically produces an effective transformation in the photon
> number basis: $\sum_n a_n |n\rangle \to \sum_n a_n |n\rangle |n\rangle$ without relying on
> conventional optical nonlinearities. Measurement of one mode enables photonic non-demolition
> detection, state preparation, and frequency conversion as applications of the same underlying
> transformation. These results establish quantum memories embedded within an interferometric
> architecture as a platform for measurement-induced photon-photon interactions and the generation
> of Gaussian and non-Gaussian entangled states.
