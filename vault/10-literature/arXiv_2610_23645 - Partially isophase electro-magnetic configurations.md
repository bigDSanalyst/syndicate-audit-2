# Partially isophase electro-magnetic configurations

## Abstract

> Separation of variables in Schrödinger equation allows an infinite-dimensional family of
> physically distinct field configurations that are dynamically indistinguishable in one selected
> sector, labeled by a conserved quantum number, while differing in all others. We call such a
> symmetry a partial isophase symmetry. Two main examples are considered: axially symmetric
> magnetic backgrounds (a rigid spectral flow of the Fock--Darwin oscillator, purely magnetic
> isophase families, and a two-channel extension) and a Cartesian slab geometry (magnetic barriers
> in two-dimensional electron gases, treated in the Appendices). We further discuss the
> consequences of this symmetry for the inverse spectral problem and consider its quasiclassical
> counterpart.
