# Hyperentanglement in intermediate phase-space planes: benchmarking a photon-pair source for quantum key distribution

## Abstract

> We characterize a source of photon pairs simultaneously entangled in polarization and in the
> transverse spatial degree of freedom, with the spatial measurements performed in intermediate
> planes of the fractional Fourier transform. The source consists of two crossed type-I crystals
> in a non-collinear geometry, such that the transverse variables carry information about the
> crystal of origin. Detection in the crystal plane reveals this information completely and
> destroys the polarization entanglement, so that a scheme using the near and far fields as
> conjugate spatial bases cannot support a parallel polarization channel. In intermediate planes
> both spatial bases combine position and momentum, such that the which-crystal information is
> partially erased, and the polarization entanglement survives in all four combinations of spatial
> bases. Applying Bell and Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen criteria to the same photon pairs certifies
> both degrees of freedom simultaneously. We evaluate quantum bit error rates and asymptotic
> secret key rates in each degree of freedom, and find that the source delivers two simultaneously
> usable channels per photon pair.
