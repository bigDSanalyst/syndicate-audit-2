# Constrained dissipation and the lifetime of quantum many-body scars

## Abstract

> We investigate the fate of quantum many-body scars in the PXP model subject to Markovian
> dissipation. We show that when local jump operators respect the same symmetries of the
> Hamiltonian the full Liouvillian spectrum displays a tower of states with properties analogous
> to standard quantum scars: large overlaps with Néel states and equally spaced gaps in the
> imaginary part of the eigenvalues, set by a single frequency. Whether the long-time dynamics is
> dominated by scars is determined by the real parts of the eigenvalues, which are set by the
> ratio between the two relaxation rates $γ_{\pm}$ describing spin flips down-to-up or up-to-down
> respectively. For asymmetric dissipation ($γ_+ > γ_-$) the scarred eigenvalues have the smallest
> real parts: the resulting enhanced fidelity oscillations are further stabilized by staggered
> jump operators. In contrast, replacing the full projection operator in the dissipators with a
> partial one breaks the symmetries of the problem and is seen to destroy dissipative scars
> altogether.
