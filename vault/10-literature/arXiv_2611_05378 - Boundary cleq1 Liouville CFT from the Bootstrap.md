# Boundary $c\leq1$ Liouville CFT from the Bootstrap

## Abstract

> We construct boundary Liouville conformal field theory at central charge $c\leq1$ with FZZT-type
> boundary conditions and show that it satisfies the Cardy--Lewellen sewing constraints on the
> disk and the annulus. Using the analytic bootstrap we derive the so far missing boundary three-
> point and bulk-to-boundary structure constants and a consistent disk one-point function as
> explicit meromorphic functions of the Liouville momenta. In sharp contrast to $c\geq25$, the
> open-string spectrum between two ``timelike'' FZZT boundaries is \emph{discrete}, yet non-
> trivially consistent through open-closed duality with the \emph{continuous} closed-string
> spectrum of Ribault and Santachiara.
