# Inaccessibility of the Cosmological Singularity to Quantum Probes

## Abstract

> We propose an operational resolution of the FLRW big bang singularity using quantum probes
> satisfying a curvature coupled Klein-Gordon equation. Applying the Madelung-Bohm decomposition
> of a Klein-Gordon field on a classical near-singularity FLRW background, we show that the
> induced conformal geometry renders curvature invariants finite and makes past directed causal
> geodesics complete. The classical big bang surface is therefore inaccessible to this class of
> quantum probes in finite effective proper time, while the underlying FLRW spacetime remains
> singular.
