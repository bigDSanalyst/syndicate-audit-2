# Annular Counterexamples and Skew-Shape Block Identities

## Abstract

> We establish four connected results on weighted order ideals, cluster coefficients, and block-
> statistic enumerators. First, we give a counterexample to Theorem 14 of Kang, Lee and Lim
> (European Journal of Combinatorics, 2026): in the rank-two annular cluster model, a single
> bridging-lamination coefficient row produces $1+q^2$, contradicting the asserted unimodality. We
> identify the unsupported initial case in their published induction. Second, we refute their log-
> concavity Conjecture 1: the same gap example violates log-concavity, and a further single-curve
> computation gives the full-support polynomial $1+2q+7q^2+10q^3+9q^4+4q^5+q^6$, which also fails
> log-concavity. Third, we establish the skew-shape block-statistic identity proposed in Kahane's
> Conjecture 4.4 (arXiv:2607.11225v1) for arbitrary finite skew Young diagrams, under an
> explicitly specified root-exclusion convention. Its proof combines commuting tableau-transfer
> matrices, a connected-block inverse kernel, and finite polynomial differential equations; the
> corresponding mathematical statements have Lean 4/Mathlib formalizations. Fourth, we derive a
> bivariate block refinement whose coefficients are nonnegative after shifting the order parameter
> by one and, when necessary, multiplying by a monomial in the weight parameter. We specify the
> correction to the printed root convention, confirmed by Kahane in correspondence, and the
> geometric sign and specialization conventions clarified by the relevant authors for the annular
> examples.
