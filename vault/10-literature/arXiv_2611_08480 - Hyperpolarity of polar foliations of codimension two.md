# Hyperpolarity of polar foliations of codimension two

## Abstract

> We consider polar foliations of codimension two on compact simply connected irreducible
> symmetric spaces. In rank at least two, we prove that these foliations are hyperpolar, settling
> the remaining case of the hyperpolarity problem discussed by Lytchak. We show that, for
> irreducible spherical Coxeter quotients, the horizontal universal cover fails to be a spherical
> building precisely on the Cayley plane, where every such foliation is congruent to one of the
> two known homogeneous models.
