# Interpretable Quantum Photonic Generative Models

## Abstract

> We propose a physical generative model for learning correlated multivariate data based on
> homodyne detection of photonic continuous-variable (CV) circuits. We use an $N$-mode photonic
> circuit in which two-mode squeezing provides correlations, local phase shifts tune the strength
> and sign of these correlations, and an adjustable interferometer redistributes them across
> modes. We train the circuit by matching its Pearson correlation matrix to the one obtained from
> data. We apply affine post-processing to adjust the marginals independently while keeping the
> relations intact. We test the model on the multivariate distribution of standardized log returns
> of financial assets, showing that the photonic model can represent rich correlation structures.
> We reproduce empirical correlations with residuals below $10^{-4}$ for five variables and
> recover the correct correlation signs for $94\% \pm 1\%$ of 190 correlation pairs at twenty
> variables, with $86\%$ of the residuals below $0.1$. As a separate technical contribution, we
> develop an approach for simulating Kerr gates with small nonlinearity via a Hubbard-Stratonovich
> expansion, making them suitable for CV-based simulators. Our results establish CV photonic
> circuits as a platform for interpretable generative learning, suitable for representing and fast
> sampling of distributions across different application domains.
