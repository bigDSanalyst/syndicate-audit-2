# The Haagerup VOA at $c=8$

## Abstract

> We announce an explicit construction of a unitary, strongly rational vertex operator algebra $U$
> of central charge $8$ inside $V_{E_6\oplus A_2}$, with a ribbon equivalence
> $\mathrm{Rep}(U)\simeq Z(\mathcal H_3)$ realizing the Haagerup double. The vertex algebra is
> generated by six affine currents and three specified weight-two fields; its weight-one Lie
> algebra is $\mathfrak{sl}_2\oplus\mathfrak{sl}_2$, at levels $1$ and $39$. We outline the
> argument, which combines exact lattice calculations, an all-weight strong-generation induction
> and $C_2$-cofiniteness, semisimple descent for a full corner of Zhu's algebra, modular Galois
> symmetry, and holomorphic $E_8$ extensions.
