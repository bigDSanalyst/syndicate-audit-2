# Observation of programmable spatiotemporal quantum-inspired bits

## Abstract

> Coherent superposition allows information to be encoded in the geometry of physical states1-7.
> Preparing individual states, however, does not determine how they can be organized throughout a
> wave, because the wave propagation and modulation constrain the underlying interference. Here we
> observe programmable spatiotemporal quantum-inspired bits in a space-time metasurface. Coherent
> synthesis from four discrete responses per polarization channel produces two-component states
> with the projective geometry of a pure quantum bit. Joint state-space synthesis retains the
> interference constraints of the shared waveform while allowing distinct polarization states to
> coexist at different resolved frequencies. Paired spatial and temporal codes organize these
> states into the prescribed trajectories, reconfigurable Stokes skyrmions and distinct
> polarizations at 48 experimentally resolved nonzero harmonic orders generated by a shared code
> pair. This connects the local state preparation to programmable field organization, establishing
> a physical basis for quantum-inspired information carriers whose encoding can exploit the
> geometrical paths, spatial winding and coordinated spectral states.
