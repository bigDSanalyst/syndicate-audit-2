# Cognitive Tours: Making Understanding a Deliverable in AI-Assisted Development

## Abstract

> Generative AI and agentic coding are accelerating software development, leading to concerns
> about technical debt. But two less visible forms of debt, cognitive debt and intent debt, are
> emerging as key risks to software health. Cognitive debt is the absence or erosion of a team's
> shared understanding where no one can confidently explain how a system works or predict the
> impact of a change. Intent debt is the lack of clearly articulated goals, constraints, and
> rationale that explain what the system is for and guide how it should evolve. This paper
> presents Cognitive Tours, a tool designed to mitigate and repair cognitive and intent debt.
> Cognitive Tours is a web-based prototype for creating and following curated paths connecting
> user needs, design decisions, code, tests, and open questions. AI-assisted skills help audit
> existing waypoints and tours for possible problems or staleness and suggest candidates for new
> ones; humans decide what matters, verify relationships, and construct the narrative. This
> division of work deliberately preserves productive friction: the sensemaking involved in
> selecting, explaining, and revising a tour, which is intended to foster and share understanding.
> Our demonstration uses a tour of Cognitive Tours itself to introduce the tool and illustrate its
> design decisions and interaction mechanisms. A proposed evaluation agenda concludes the paper. A
> demo is available at https://cognitive-tours-demo.vercel.app/
