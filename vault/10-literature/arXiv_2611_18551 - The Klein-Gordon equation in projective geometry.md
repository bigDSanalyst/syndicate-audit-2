# The Klein-Gordon equation in projective geometry

## Abstract

> We discuss a geometric method by which Hörmander's result on the asymptotics of a Klein-Gordon
> field, on an asymptotically flat spacetime, can be related to some partial peeling, à la
> Penrose, along an extended projective boundary, embedded in a spacetime with one dimension
> higher.
