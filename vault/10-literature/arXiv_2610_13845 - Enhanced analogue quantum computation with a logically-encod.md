# Enhanced analogue quantum computation with a logically-encoded XX gadget

## Abstract

> The introduction of two-body XX interactions has been shown to improve the performance of
> quantum annealing in solving certain combinatorial optimisation problems and would enable the
> simulation of a broader class of many-body Hamiltonians. Due to experimental constraints, such
> interactions are typically not natively available on analogue quantum devices. To overcome this
> limitation we introduce the truss gadget. This is an exact construction that generates two-body
> XX interactions using only one-body X and Z fields and two-body ZZ interactions which are
> readily accessible on, for instance, superconducting quantum annealers. We experimentally
> validate the construction by reproducing the dynamics of a target XX Hamiltonian on a trapped-
> ion quantum processor. We then apply the gadget to an illustrative set of instances of the
> weighted maximum-independent-set problem on a superconducting quantum annealer. We empirically
> determine an approximate thousand-fold improvement in the probability of finding a state close
> to the ground state for these instances, over implementation without an XX interaction.
