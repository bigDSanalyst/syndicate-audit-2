# On the multiplicity of crossing limit cycles of double diagonalizable piecewise linear vector fields

## Abstract

> We consider planar piecewise linear vector fields with two zones separated by a straight line
> and having two diagonalizable components, which we call the \emph{double diagonalizable
> configuration}. In this configuration, the Poincaré half-maps correspond to separatrices of
> saddle points in the foliations of symmetric differential forms $ω_r(x)-ω_r(y)$, where $ω_r$ is
> a rational $1$-form with exactly three simple poles. Limit cycles correspond to non-diagonal
> intersections of two such separatrices, and the multiplicity of a limit cycle is precisely the
> order of contact at the intersection point. We prove that two distinct separatrices which are
> not nested segments of the same straight line have a contact of order at most $4$ at a diagonal
> point and at most $2$ at a non-diagonal point. As a consequence, we obtain that the multiplicity
> of limit cycles in the double diagonalizable configuration is at most two, this bound being
> sharp.
