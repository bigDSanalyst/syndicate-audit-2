# Traces of Vacuum Squeezing: A Superconducting Transmission-Line Analogue of Cosmological Particle Creation

## Abstract

> Particle creation in time-dependent quantum fields is a central phenomenon in early-universe
> cosmology, yet it remains experimentally inaccessible in its original context. We show
> theoretically that superconducting circuits based on a waveguide-like transmission line loaded
> with dc-SQUIDs can provide a direct analogue of a scalar field in a radiation-dominated
> Friedmann--Robertson--Walker universe in conformal time. The mode equation of the circuit
> reduces to the Weber equation, the same equation governing cosmological particle creation in a
> radiation-dominated background, admitting an exact solution in terms of parabolic cylinder
> functions and an exponentially suppressed particle spectrum. We analyse the conditions under
> which the analogy holds, which require a compromise between the duration of the pulse, the
> uniformity of the modulation and the symmetry of the SQUIDs, and show that dissipation does not
> erase the cosmological signal. We use the anomalous moment of each mode as a witness of the
> Bogoliubov mixing: it vanishes for any thermal state and survives dissipation, and, together
> with the photon number, it certifies squeezing, and with the purity of the state, it identifies
> whether the pairs created from the vacuum dominate.
