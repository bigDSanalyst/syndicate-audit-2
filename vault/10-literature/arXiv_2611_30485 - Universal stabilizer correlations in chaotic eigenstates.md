# Universal stabilizer correlations in chaotic eigenstates

## Abstract

> Entanglement and nonstabilizerness provide complementary perspectives on quantum-state
> structure. In this work, we find that the stabilizer correlation, defined as the difference
> between the Rényi-$2$ mutual information and the mutual stabilizer Rényi entropy, approaches a
> universal constant for chaotic eigenstates. This constant is determined solely by the
> symmetries, and quantifies the correlation that does not contribute to nonstabilizerness. We
> identify this universal stabilizer correlation analytically via random-state ensembles, which
> give $\log_2 7$ (GOE) and $\log_2 4$(GUE) without extra symmetries, $\log_2 14$ and $\log_2 8$
> under spin-inversion parity or half-filling magnetization. While imposing reflection or
> translation symmetry leaves these values unchanged. Exact calculations for a chaotic mixed-field
> Ising chain confirm the predicted plateau and finite-size approach. Integrable free-fermion and
> strongly disordered eigenstates behave qualitatively differently. This universal stabilizer
> correlation reveals a characteristic structure of chaotic eigenstates beyond entanglement or
> nonstabilizerness alone.
