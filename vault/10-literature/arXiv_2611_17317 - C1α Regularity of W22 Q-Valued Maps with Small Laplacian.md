# $C^{1,α}$ Regularity of $W^{2,2}$ $Q$-Valued Maps with Small Laplacian

## Abstract

> We establish a regularity criterion for $Q$-valued maps $u\in W^{2,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}(Ω,\mathcal
> A_Q(\mathbb R^k))$, where $Ω\subset\mathbb R^n$ is open. If $|Δu|\leqδ_*|D^2u|$ almost
> everywhere, with $δ_*=δ_*(n,k,Q)>0$ sufficiently small, then $u\in C^{1,α}_{\mathrm{loc}}$ for
> some $α=α(n,k,Q)>0$, with quantitative estimates. The result holds for arbitrary $n\geq2$,
> $k\geq1$, and $Q\geq1$. The proof establishes uniform energy decay using a rigidity principle
> for positive-semidefinite matrix-valued measures with an aperture bound and approximately atomic
> divergence, extending the target-stress argument for the homogeneity gap in \cite{Luc26}. The
> argument relies only on first variation identities. As consequences, we obtain $C^{1,α}$
> regularity for Dirichlet-stationary $W^{2,2}$ maps and for locally Lipschitz $W^{2,2}$ maps
> whose graph varifolds are stationary and have sufficiently small slope. Combining the latter
> result with Bellettini's Lipschitz sheeting theorem \cite{Bel25}, we establish $C^{1,α}$
> sheeting in the multiple-valued sense for two-sided stable minimal immersions whose singular set
> has vanishing $2$-capacity relative to the immersion, under suitable flatness and mass bounds.
> Our results allow us to reduce the proof of compactness within this class, under locally uniform
> mass bounds, to the conjecture that branch sets of stationary $C^{1,α}$ multiple-valued graphs
> in codimension one have vanishing $2$-capacity.
