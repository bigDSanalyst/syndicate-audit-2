# Uniform restriction lower bounds and nongeodesic common nodal curves in integrable systems

## Abstract

> We construct a family of real analytic metrics on the two-torus, for which a fixed embedded
> nongeodesic circle is a nodal component of infinitely many real Laplace eigenfunctions. The
> examples address a question asked by Bourgain-Rudnick \cite{BR11}, and give a negative answer to
> the question asked by Toth-Zelditch \cite{TZ21} and Han \cite{Han}: whether a curve shared by
> infinitely many nodal sets must be geodesic. To explain the restriction obstruction, we prove
> uniform $L^2$ lower bounds for joint eigenfunctions of quantum completely integrable (QCI)
> systems under transverse crossing and isolation of a tangential characteristic branch, then
> extend to multi-branch setting. The results are used to characterize the possible joint nodal
> curves in the Liouville tori, narrowing down the choices. Finally, the counterexample realizes
> one possibility for which cancellation happens exactly on a nongeodesic curve.
