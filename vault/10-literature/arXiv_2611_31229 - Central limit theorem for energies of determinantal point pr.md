# Central limit theorem for energies of determinantal point processes on complex manifolds I

## Abstract

> We study the Bergman determinantal point process associated with a positive line bundle on a
> compact Riemann surface. Consider the U-statistic given by the Green function, called the Green
> energy. For a configuration of $N$ points, the expectation of Green energy is $\frac12 N\log
> N+O(N)$, and the variance is $(\log 2-π^2/24)N+O(\log^2 N)$. We prove a central limit theorem
> for the Green energy by combining local Bergman kernel asymptotics with an expansion for the
> cumulants. A central limit theorem also holds for the logarithmic energy of Bergman
> determinantal point process on complex manifolds.
