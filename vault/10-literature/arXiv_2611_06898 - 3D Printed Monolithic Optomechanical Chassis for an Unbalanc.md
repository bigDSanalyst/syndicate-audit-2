# 3D Printed Monolithic Optomechanical Chassis for an Unbalanced Field-Widened Interferometer

## Abstract

> Additive manufacturing is increasingly being explored as an approach for producing complex
> structures for optical hardware. In this work, we present a fully monolithic optomechanical
> chassis fabricated using laser powder bed fusion additive manufacturing. The structure was
> fabricated from thermally stable Invar36, a low-thermal-expansion alloy that enables high
> dimensional stability under varying environmental conditions. Our monolithic design incorporates
> embedded optical mounts, flexures, and alignment features directly into the chassis geometry
> that exceed the practical limits of conventional subtractive manufacturing. By integrating the
> optomechanical support structure into a single compact unit, the system achieves a reduced
> footprint compared to traditional optomechanical assemblies. The results demonstrate the
> potential of additive manufacturing to produce lightweight, and highly customized optical
> systems, opening the possibility of new pathways for next-generation quantum sensors and optical
> instrumentation.
