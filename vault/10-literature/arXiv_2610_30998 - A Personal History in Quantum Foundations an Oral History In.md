# A Personal History in Quantum Foundations: an Oral History Interview

## Abstract

> This oral history interview provides Yakir Aharonov's personal and conceptual recollections from
> the 1950s to the 2000s, focusing on his academic trajectory, some of his major contributions to
> quantum foundations, and his perspective on the development of the field. The interview traces
> Aharonov's academic career through the Technion, University of Bristol, Brandeis University,
> Yeshiva University, University of South Carolina, Tel Aviv University, George Mason University,
> and Chapman University. The interview also traces the development of Aharonov's work and
> collaborations on the EPR paradox, time-energy uncertainty relations, quantum measurement
> theory, quantum frames of reference, the two-state vector formalism, weak values, protective
> measurements, and several other contributions. It includes recollections of meetings and
> discussions with David Bohm, Niels Bohr, John Clauser, Paul Dirac, Rudolf Peierls, Aage
> Petersen, and Abner Shimony. Also mentioned: David Albert, Jeeva Anandan, Peter Bergmann, Gideon
> Carmi, Avshalom Elitzur, Dave Finkelstein, Vladimir Fock, Itzhak Kalugai, Ed Lerner, Joel
> Lebowitz, Stanley Mandelstam, Yuval Ne'eman, Sandu Popescu, Maurice Pryce, Daniel Rohrlich,
> Nathan Rosen, Joseph Safko, Paul Singer, Daniele Struppa, Leonard Susskind, Lev Vaidman, Chien-
> Shiung Wu.
