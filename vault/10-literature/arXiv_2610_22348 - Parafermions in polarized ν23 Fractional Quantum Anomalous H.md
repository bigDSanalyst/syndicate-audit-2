# Parafermions in polarized $ν=2/3$ Fractional Quantum (Anomalous) Hall Heterostructures

## Abstract

> Motivated by recent experimental signatures of fractional Chern insulators in 2D quantum
> materials, we investigate the realization of $\mathbb Z_3$ parafermion zero modes (PZMs) on
> edges of spin-polarized $ν=2/3$ fractional quantum Hall states which are proximitized by
> superconductors. Unlike the spin-unpolarized Halperin state, the polarized $ν=2/3$ edge contains
> counterpropagating electron and fractional quasihole modes, giving rise to a plethora of
> competing tunneling and pairing processes. We focus on the vicinity of the fixed point where
> these modes reorganize into decoupled charge and neutral sectors, with the neutral sector
> describing an emergent dipole degree of freedom. Using bosonization and renormalization group
> analysis, we lay out the parameter space in which the interedge dipole interactions gap the
> neutral sector to stabilize one dominant tunneling and one dominant pairing channel resulting in
> a double sine-Gordon model in the charge sector which supports $\mathbb{Z}_3$ PZMs at domain
> walls. We discuss both clean and disordered systems. In clean systems this mechanism requires a
> certain degree of control of the interaction strength and range. In contrast, disorder can
> provide a more generic route: While short-ranged, edge-uncorrelated disorder % on the two edges
> suppresses the simultaneous pinning required for stable parafermion modes, when the disorder on
> the two edges is sufficiently correlated, as expected for smooth disorder potentials, the
> parameter regime hosting PZMs increases.
