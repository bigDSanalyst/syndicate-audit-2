# On the coincidence of discrete and continuous definitions of Entanglement of Formation

## Abstract

> It is proved that every Borel ensemble of pure states on a separable bipartite Hilbert space
> with finite average entanglement admits a countable ensemble with the same barycenter and
> arbitrarily small additional average entanglement. Consequently, the discrete and continuous
> versions of the Entanglement of Formation coincide on the whole space of states of infinite-
> dimensional bipartite quantum system (with infinite values allowed). The main idea of the proof
> (realized with the AI-assistance) is the identification of a Borel ensemble of pure bipartite
> states with a Borel probability measure on the unit sphere of the space of Hilbert--Schmidt
> operators followed by dividing the support of this measure into appropriate cells and replacing
> each cell by its conditional barycenter. The resulting countable ensemble is obtained by a
> recursive construction.
