# From pure spinor modules to superspace superfields

## Abstract

> We relate the pure spinor formalism to the superspace description of constrained superfields and
> gauge multiplets, mainly for abelian two-dimensional N=(2,2) supersymmetry, with three- and
> four-dimensional N=1 examples for comparison. For each multiplet we give an explicit module over
> the supertranslation Chevalley-Eilenberg algebra and match its reconstruction with the known
> superfields. First-order constraints come from modules supported on linear subspaces of the
> nilpotence variety, and higher-order ones, such as the complex-linear constraint, from Yoneda
> products of their Ext classes. Requiring a connection to act on given matter and its conjugate
> restricts its curvature, which yields the ordinary, twisted and semichiral vector multiplets
> with their field strengths. The module of each vector multiplet is the fiber product over the
> origin of the coordinate rings of the matter supports: its potentials are differences of partial
> potentials, and its gauge parameters are the matter superfields. For the large vector multiplet
> this fiber product is not a quotient of the pure spinor ring; its field strengths are four odd
> semichiral superfields, and partial regluing gives its constrained versions.
