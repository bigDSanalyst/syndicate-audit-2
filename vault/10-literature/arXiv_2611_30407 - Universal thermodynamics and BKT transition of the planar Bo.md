# Universal thermodynamics and BKT transition of the planar Bose gas from weak to strong interactions

## Abstract

> We compute the universal thermodynamics of the dilute two-dimensional Bose gas at weak and
> strong coupling using the functional renormalization group (FRG). We employ an approximation
> that retains the full field dependence of the effective potential and spatial derivative terms,
> extending previous FRG calculations. We find that the equation of state is remarkably
> insensitive to the approximation scheme, with differences of only a few percent even at strong
> coupling. By contrast, the improved approximation substantially improves the description of the
> Berezinskii-Kosterlitz-Thouless (BKT) transition, reproducing the universal jump of the
> superfluid density and the critical anomalous dimension to within a few percent. At weak
> coupling, our results agree very well with classical-field Monte Carlo simulations, while at
> stronger coupling we observe deviations from classical-field scaling, valid at weak coupling.
> Our approach thus provides a quantitative description of the thermodynamics and superfluid
> density across the BKT transition, including in the strong-coupling regime where classical-field
> methods are no longer reliable.
