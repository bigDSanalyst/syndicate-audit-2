# Global bound-state spectrum and spin- and angular-momentum-dependent confinement in an inverse-$3/2$ magnetic quantum dot

## Abstract

> We investigate the bound-state spectrum and confinement thresholds of a two-dimensional electron
> governed by the Schrödinger--Pauli equation in an inverse-$3/2$ radial magnetic-field profile.
> The radial problem reduces to the biconfluent Heun equation, but instead of restricting the
> solution to the quasi-exact polynomial sector, we impose the bound-state condition globally
> through a spectral Wronskian between the endpoint-selected Frobenius solution and the
> subdominant asymptotic solution. The resulting spectrum contains continuous nonpolynomial bound-
> state branches on which the known quasi-exact solutions appear as isolated exact points. In this
> global description, the polynomial index $N$ remains a termination index within the quasi-exact
> sector but does not uniquely label the spectral branches. An independent exact analysis of the
> continuum edge yields spin- and angular-momentum-dependent critical couplings that can lie below
> the quasi-exact restriction $U_0a>4$, where $U_0$ is the Coulomb-strength parameter and $a$ is
> the magnetic-profile length scale. These results establish quasi-exact solvability as a set of
> special analytic points embedded in a broader biconfluent-Heun spectrum with spin- and angular-
> momentum-dependent confinement thresholds.
