# On Higher-Form Fracton Hydrodynamics with Momentum Conservation

## Abstract

> Momentum conservation is common in fluids, yet it may be incompatible with the hydrodynamics of
> theories with exotic conservation laws (such as fracton hydrodynamics) unless symmetries are
> spontaneously broken. Using the Martin-Siggia-Rose (MSR) effective field theory formalism, we
> develop a representation-theoretic framework for the hydrodynamics of translationally invariant
> systems with exotic conservation laws without spontaneous symmetry breaking. Under mild
> assumptions on the form of hydrodynamics, we show that such theories must satisfy two
> representation theoretic consistency conditions and a generalized Gauss's law constraint.
> Higher-form symmetries satisfy these conditions and we derive their ideal hydrodynamics; in
> three dimensions, the one-form case produces ideal non-relativistic magnetohydrodynamics.
> Finally, we prove that no other theories are compatible with momentum conservation in SO(3) nor
> SO($d$), for sufficiently large odd $d$.
