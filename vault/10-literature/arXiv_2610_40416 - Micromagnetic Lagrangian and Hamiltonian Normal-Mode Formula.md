# Micromagnetic Lagrangian and Hamiltonian Normal-Mode Formulation of Cavity Magnon-Photon Interactions

## Abstract

> We investigate magnon-photon interactions in a resonant cavity starting from the coupled Maxwell
> and Landau-Lifshitz equations linearized about an arbitrary micromagnetic ground state. By
> introducing an electric vector potential and a suitable interaction Lagrangian, the formulation
> avoids the cancellation of large magnetostatic contributions that arises in the conventional
> magnetic-vector-potential description and becomes inaccurate under modal truncation. The
> magnetization and cavity fields are expanded in the normal modes of the uncoupled magnetic and
> electromagnetic subsystems, with coupling coefficients given by overlap integrals of the
> corresponding eigenfunctions. The resulting multimode Lagrangian has a noncanonical structure
> due to the gyrotropic terms, and the corresponding Hamiltonian formulation is obtained using the
> Faddeev-Jackiw approach. The radiative magnon-photon interaction is encoded in the resulting
> Poisson-bracket structure, while the cavity-induced modification of the magnetostatic field
> enters explicitly in the Hamiltonian. The coupled system is diagonalized through a generalized
> Bogoliubov transformation, yielding canonical polariton variables that provide a natural basis
> for quantization. Application to a perpendicularly magnetized YIG disk in a rectangular
> microwave cavity shows hybridization between the cavity mode and several magnetostatic modes,
> together with appreciable cavity-wall-induced frequency shifts when the sample is placed close
> to the conducting walls.
