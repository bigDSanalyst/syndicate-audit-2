# Electrons in a hard-wall cylinder with helicoidal metric: time-reversal pairing, cubic anticrossings, and twist-activated dipole transitions

## Abstract

> We study the scalar Schrödinger equation in a helicoidal metric when the axial direction is
> closed by Dirichlet walls built into the operator domain. With hard walls, the axial derivative
> cannot be replaced by a traveling-wave factor, and in a rotationally symmetric spinless cavity,
> time reversal makes the levels with azimuthal numbers $m$ and $-m$ exactly degenerate. We write
> the Hamiltonian as a Hermitian coupled-subband matrix and give an exact gauge-adapted
> variational representation. For a finite radius, the leading twist correction is quadratic, and
> changes sign at a critical aspect ratio; for an unbounded radial domain, it is linear in
> $|Ω_g|$. Retuning the aspect ratio through an isolated radial--axial resonance within one $m$
> sector opens an avoided crossing with gap $Δ_{\mathrm{gap}}/E_R=1.648775\ldots|Ω_gR^2/L|^3$ to
> leading order. A transverse dipole transition forbidden without twist acquires an oscillator
> strength quadratic in the twist, with leading coefficient $0.0559408010$ for $R/L=2$ from
> perturbation theory. Dipole sum rules and the convergence of the currents serve as checks on the
> numerics. We also give the inverse-mass tensor required for a scalar realization and re-examine
> each statement and figure of the earlier article [Opt. Quantum Electron. \textbf{58}, 113
> (2026)] within the corrected model.
