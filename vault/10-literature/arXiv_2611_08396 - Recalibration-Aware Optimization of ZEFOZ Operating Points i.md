# Recalibration-Aware Optimization of ZEFOZ Operating Points in $^{167}\mathrm{Er}^{3+}:\mathrm{Y}_2\mathrm{SiO}_5$ for Quantum Memories

## Abstract

> Erbium-doped solids like $^{167}\mathrm{Er}^{3+}:\mathrm{Y}_2\mathrm{SiO}_5$ have properties
> that make them suitable for quantum-network memories, however, their unquenched effective
> electronic spin causes rapid magnetic decoherence. Operating at Zero First-Order Zeeman (ZEFOZ)
> points allows us to mitigate this, where we evaluate the ZEFOZ landscape for Site-2
> $^{167}\mathrm{Er}^{3+}$ by incorporating a full-Hessian spin-Hamiltonian treatment with the
> anisotropic magnetic-field covariance derived from the native $^{89}\mathrm{Y}$ crystal lattice
> directly. Operating-point screening criteria were adopted to devise a Pareto-optimal frontier of
> four transition pairs to balance modeled Y-only magnetic pure-dephasing lifetimes
> ($T_{φ,Y}^{(2)}$) against microwave control authority. The $ε_{14}\leftrightarrowε_{15}$
> transition exhibits the longest modeled Y-bath-induced quadratic pure-dephasing time,
> $T_{φ,Y}^{(2)}>2.2\times10^5~\mathrm{s}$, while the $ε_{12}\leftrightarrowε_{13}$ transition
> stood out, achieving a Rabi frequency coefficient of $1.248 \times 10^5~\mathrm{Hz/mT}$. An
> assumption-based Hamiltonian-uncertainty analysis demonstrates that while uncompensated
> deviations shift the ZEFOZ condition away from its nominal magnetic-field coordinate, the
> shifted operating points can be recovered via numerical vector-field recalibration. The Pareto-
> optimal coordinates were mathematically recovered for all sampled Hamiltonian perturbations, and
> the $84.1^{\mathrm{st}}$-percentile field relocations ranged from $10.95$ to
> $27.97~\mathrm{mT}$. The intended operation near $10~\mathrm{mK}$ must be taken into account for
> the suppression of Er-induced spectral diffusion through electronic-spin polarization, while the
> extended Y-bath pure-dephasing timescales also remain conditional on the adopted temporal-
> correlation model.
