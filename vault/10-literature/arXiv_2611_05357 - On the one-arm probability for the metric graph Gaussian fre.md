# On the one-arm probability for the metric graph Gaussian free field in high dimensions

## Abstract

> We consider the sign clusters of the Gaussian free field on the cable system of a transient
> weighted graph with volume growth exponent $α$ and Green function decay exponent $ν$. We prove
> that the probability that the cluster of a vertex reaches distance $R$ is of order $R^{-β}$ when
> $2α<3ν$, where $β=α-ν$ is the walk dimension, and that the same holds up to a logarithmic factor
> in the upper bound when $2α=3ν$. On $\mathbb Z^d$ these conditions read $d>6$ and $d=6$. The
> upper bound combines a general entropic bound, which compares the law of the cluster under the
> critical loop soup and under a massive one, with a Simon-Lieb-type inequality for the massive
> field derived from its Markov property. The arguments are robust and require no transitivity.
> The lower bound follows from a competition between the capacity and the volume of the cluster.
> In both bounds, the key quantity is the expected exit time of the random walk from balls, which
> links the volume of the cluster to its radius.
