# Performance analysis of a Rydberg atom THz radiometer concept

## Abstract

> THz receiver technology lies between electronics and photonics; as such, its implementation is
> not as mature. This warrants investigation into alternative THz receivers such as Rydberg atom
> sensors, which are highly susceptible to electric fields. In particular, the conversion
> architecture has been demonstrated to coherently transduce thermal radiation. This system offers
> room-temperature operation and does not necessarily require a local oscillator at the frequency
> of the input signal. We provide an assessment of the feasibility of such sensors for THz
> radiometry within the context of Earth observation.
