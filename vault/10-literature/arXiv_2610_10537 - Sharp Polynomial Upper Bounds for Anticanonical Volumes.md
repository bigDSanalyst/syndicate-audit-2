# Sharp Polynomial Upper Bounds for Anticanonical Volumes

## Abstract

> For every positive integer $n$, we prove that there is a constant $C_n$, depending only on $n$,
> such that $Vol(-K_X) \leq C_n ε^{-(2^n-n-1)}$ whenever $X$ admits an $ε$-lc log Fano boundary.
> The exponent is optimal in every dimension at least two, even for toric Fano varieties of Picard
> number one. In particular, the optimal exponent for fourfolds is eleven. We also prove an
> anticanonical interpolation theorem with optimal exponent $2^n-1$. The proof combines signed
> discrepancy estimates under projection, finite morphisms to projective space, and the canonical
> bundle formula. A reduction to a base bounded independently of $ε$, followed by a volume
> estimate along a flag, yields the sharper volume exponent.
