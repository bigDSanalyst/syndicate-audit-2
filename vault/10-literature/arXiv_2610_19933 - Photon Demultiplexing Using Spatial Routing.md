# Photon Demultiplexing Using Spatial Routing

## Abstract

> Spatial sectioning of the far-field noncollinear field from spontaneous parametric down-
> conversion (SPDC) is a promising approach for multiparty quantum communication. Although
> demonstrations with six pairs of users have been reported, the fundamental limits remain
> unknown. These include the maximum number of channels, the required degree of channel
> optimization, and the dependence on input parameters. In this paper, we provide a practical
> framework that answers these questions by connecting input parameters and invoking a design
> theorem. For a representative configuration, we realize 12 user-pair channels with routing
> consistency exceeding $0.9999$ at a $12\%$ reduction in photon flux. We further show that
> sectioning improves the accidental-coincidence budget linearly with the number of channels $N$,
> allowing the aggregate key rate to scale with the user count. These results provide a design
> strategy for spatial-division multiplexing of entangled photons analogous to the channel-spacing
> standards used in wavelength-division networks.
