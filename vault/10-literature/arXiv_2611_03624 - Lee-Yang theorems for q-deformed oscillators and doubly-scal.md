# Lee-Yang theorems for $q$-deformed oscillators and doubly-scaled SYK

## Abstract

> We show that the vacuum partition function of the $q$-deformed oscillator \[ L_q(z):=
> \langle0|e^{zT_q}|0\rangle, \qquad T_q := \mathsf{a}_q + \mathsf{a}_q^* \] satisfies the Lee-
> Yang property, i.e., all zeros of $L_q$ lie on the imaginary line for all $q\in[-1, 1)$, proving
> the conjectures of Okuyama'23. Using the connection between $q$-deformed oscillators and the
> Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev (SYK) Hamiltonian in the doubly-scaled regime, we show that the annealed SYK
> partition function satisfies the Lee-Yang property in the thermodynamic limit, that is, all of
> its Fisher zeros are on the imaginary axis. As a result, we show that the quenched SYK partition
> function admits a zero-free strip of constant width with high probability, rigorously
> complementing the large$-k$ replica calculations of Zlokapa and Kiani'26. We show the imaginary-
> rootedness of $L_q$ by expressing $\varphi_q(z):= L_q(\mathbf{i} z)$ as a locally uniform limit
> of ``$q$-deformed'' matching polynomials and then showing that those $q$-deformed polynomials
> are real-rooted. This establishes that $\varphi_q$ is real-rooted, and in fact belongs to the
> \emph{Laguerre-Pólya} class. The real-rootedness of the $q$-deformed polynomials yields novel
> log-concavity relations on the \emph{$q$-deformed matching moments} \[M_\ell(q):=
> \sum_{π\in\mathcal{P}_2(2\ell)}q^{\operatorname{cr}(π)}.\] We show the real-rootedness of the
> $q$-deformed polynomials by using the Touchard--Riordan formula to derive a partial differential
> equation relating their $q$-derivatives to their $z$-derivatives, and then analyzing the
> resulting motion of their roots.
