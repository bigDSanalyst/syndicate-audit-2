# Black hole radial geometry from a quantum spin chain

## Abstract

> We show that the radial differential equation for a scalar field in an anti-de Sitter black
> brane background can be encoded in an exactly solvable semi-infinite spin chain independent of
> dimensionality. The radial coordinate emerges as lattice quasimomentum. Spin coherence encodes
> the geometry, while the spectrum and response recover gravitational observables and yield exact
> screening-mass sum rules. We implement the finite chain on a superconducting processor. At
> finite filling and low energies, the model yields a conformal field theory in a coordinate
> proportional to proper radial distance.
