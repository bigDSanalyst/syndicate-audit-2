# A Microwave-Photonics Formulation of the Rydberg-Atom Receiver

## Abstract

> The performance of Rydberg-atom receivers is seldom stated as a radio-frequency (RF) link
> budget. The receiver is shown to be an intensity-modulated direct-detection analog optical link
> whose modulator is the laser-driven vapor. The transmission of a warm vapor is derived in closed
> form by averaging the four-level response over atomic velocities. The conversion gain, noise
> figure, bandwidth, intercept points, and spurious-free dynamic range follow from this one
> function. For a weak probe the instantaneous bandwidth is half the width of the transparency
> window in two-photon detuning. The local-oscillator beat places the second-order intercept near
> twice the local-oscillator field, so the local oscillator sets the even-order linearity. For a
> rubidium transition at 17.04 GHz the closed forms agree with a velocity-resolved density-matrix
> model to 1% for the slope and the intercepts and to 10% for the bandwidth. They underestimate
> the group delay by a third. The response is tied to atomic constants, and the sensing volume
> escapes the Chu limit. However, the noise figure of a warm vapor rules it out as a general
> replacement for an antenna and low-noise amplifier.
