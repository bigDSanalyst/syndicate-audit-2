# For whom the Bell tolls: joint measurements and state updates in quantum field theory

## Abstract

> State update rules and their behaviour in Bell tests play an important role in the study of
> measurement schemes and quantum information. We consider the state updates of the Fewster-Verch
> measurement framework for quantum field theory using their connection to the probability
> distribution of measurement outcomes. We focus on a set of observables of the form
> $A(f_i)^*A(f_i)$, where $A(f)$ is the (complex) smeared field operator for any theory induced by
> a formally hermitian, Green hyperbolic differential operator, and where the $(A(f_i))_{i=1,
> \dots, m}$ are assumed to be normal and mutually commuting. Under the assumption that these
> operators have a sufficiently nice representation on a bosonic Fock space, we derive the joint
> moment generating functional and the unique joint probability distribution for the observables
> $(A(f_i)^*A(f_i))_{i=1,\dots,m}$. As an application of these results, we construct a model of a
> Bell inequality test within quantum field theory using a recently developed coupled Proca-scalar
> model for polarization measurements. We show that our Bell test model can violate the CHSH
> inequality, approaching Tsirelson's bound for maximally entangled states in the limit where
> vacuum effects are perfectly rejected. These results show that the state update rules derived in
> the Fewster-Verch framework are compatible with strongly quantum correlations. They also support
> a viewpoint in which state updates should be understood in relation to available information.
