# Testing the Spin of New Physics with Top Precision Measurements

## Abstract

> Precision measurements can reveal not only the presence of new physics, but also information
> about the dynamics that generated it. We study this question in top-pair production by comparing
> three top-philic scenarios: a colored scalar top partner, a colored vector-like fermion, and a
> spin-one $Z^\prime$ mediator. The first two contribute through loop corrections, while the
> $Z^\prime$ contributes at tree level. We show that the scalar and vector-like fermion scenarios
> match onto the same effective operator structure at low energies,making their distinction
> unfeasible using the EFT approach. However, near or above threshold their full loop amplitudes
> develop distinct momentum-dependent interference patterns. Using the top transverse momentum
> spectrum we find that resonant and loop-induced effects are relatively easy to distinguish,
> whereas separating the scalar and vector-like hypotheses is substantially more challenging.
> Nevertheless, their different analytic structures leave measurable information in the shape of
> the distribution. These results illustrate how precision differential measurements can probe the
> spin and dynamics of virtual new states even when they are not directly reconstructed, and
> motivate more powerful multidimensional analyses.
