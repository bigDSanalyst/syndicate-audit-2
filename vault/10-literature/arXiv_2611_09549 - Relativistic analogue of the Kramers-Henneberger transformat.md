# Relativistic analogue of the Kramers-Henneberger transformation and pair creation

## Abstract

> In analogy to the Kramers-Henneberger transformation in non-relativistic quantum mechanics, we
> propose and investigate a spinor transformation for the Dirac equation which allows us to
> transform space-time dependent vector potentials $A(t,x)\boldsymbol{e}$_x away. In the mass-less
> case, we exactly arrive at the free Dirac equation, while for massive Dirac fields, the
> transformation results in a non-trivial mass matrix containing space-time dependent phases.
> Special emphasis is placed on the phenomenon of fermion pair creation in the external field
> $A(t,x)\boldsymbol{e}_x$ where the spinor transformation allows us to conceptually disentangle
> the different contributions to pair creation. It can also be used to reformulate the WKB
> approach and the Riccati equation (which can be advantageous in certain cases) and to obtain
> additional insight into the mechanism of dynamically assisted pair creation.
