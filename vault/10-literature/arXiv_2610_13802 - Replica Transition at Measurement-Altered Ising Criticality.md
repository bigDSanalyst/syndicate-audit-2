# Replica Transition at Measurement-Altered Ising Criticality

## Abstract

> Performing measurements on gapless quantum ground states can give rise to new universal
> phenomena, whose origins remain poorly understood even for the paradigmatic Ising critical
> point. Replica methods offer a natural framework to study such post-measurement ensembles, with
> the replica limit $Q\to 1$ describing physical Born-averaged observables. Here we show that, for
> the monitored critical Ising chain, the replicated post-measurement ensemble of states itself
> undergoes a phase transition precisely at the physically relevant replica limit $Q \to 1$,
> preventing standard replica treatments. We use boundary conformal field theory (BCFT) methods to
> explain the underlying mechanism of this transition. Our methods yield a perturbative analytic
> calculation of the universal $O(1)$ constant in the Shannon entropy of the measurement record,
> previously known only numerically. We also apply our tools to study measurement-averaged spin
> correlators, providing a unified description of the universal properties of measurement outcomes
> and post-measurement states in the critical Ising chain.
