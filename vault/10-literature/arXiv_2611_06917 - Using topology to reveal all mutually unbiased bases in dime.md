# Using topology to reveal all mutually unbiased bases in dimension six

## Abstract

> Mutually unbiased bases (MUBs) describe complementary quantum measurements: certainty in one
> basis gives no information about the others. In dimension six, triples of MUBs are known, but
> whether a fourth basis could exist remained unresolved for over three decades. OpenAI recently
> reported a proof that none exists. Here we use the topology of the space of complex Hadamard
> matrices, which describe transitions between MUBs, to characterize all sets in dimension six. We
> prove that every mutually unbiased triple belongs to a two-parameter family or is a single
> isolated triple built from the Fourier matrix. Since none of them extend to a fourth basis, this
> also proves that no four MUBs exist. Our proof rests on a new topological result that all order-
> six complex Hadamard matrices except Tao's form a connected space. Connectedness reduces the
> classification of triples to finitely many overlapping four-dimensional phase balls. We certify
> with interval arithmetic that these balls cover the space and for every pair of MUBs represented
> in a ball, we then determine all third bases that extend it. Dimension six is now the first non-
> prime-power dimension in which all sets of MUBs are classified.
