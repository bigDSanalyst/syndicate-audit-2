# Near-Quantum-Limited Readout for the Princeton Axion Search using a sub-GHz Kinetic Inductance Traveling Wave Parametric Amplifier

## Abstract

> Parametric amplifiers are a workhorse for near-quantum-limited measurements above 1 GHz.
> However, amplifier development has been more limited below 1 GHz. We present a kinetic
> inductance traveling-wave parametric amplifier (KI-TWPA) operating over 300-700 MHz, with gain
> exceeding 30 dB and pump powers of a few hundred nanowatts. The system noise performance, as
> measured by a variable-temperature noise source, is 2.5-3.5 quanta. This device is a versatile
> new tool for precision measurements of sub-GHz signals, with applications including readout of
> semiconductor spin qubits, superconducting detector arrays, and searches for new physics beyond
> the Standard Model. We characterize aspects of the amplifier performance relevant to axion
> searches, including gain drift, compression point, amplifier noise at various operating
> temperatures, readout with an input circulator, and magnetic field insensitivity.
