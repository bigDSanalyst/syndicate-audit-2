# A proof of the chemical recurrence conjecture in two dimensions

## Abstract

> At the microscopic level, the dynamics of a network of chemical reactions can be modeled, in the
> well-mixed case, as a continuous-time Markov chain. This process counts the number of particles
> in each of the $d$ species as a function of time, and its state-dependent, polynomial jump rates
> are determined by the structure of the underlying reaction graph. Networks of chemical reactions
> whose reaction graphs are the union of strongly connected components are called \emph{weakly
> reversible}. The long-standing \emph{chemical recurrence conjecture} states that if a network is
> weakly reversible, then the associated Markov chain is positive recurrent. We prove this
> conjecture for $d=2$.
