# PyRUA-Dex: Learning Programmatic Policies for Dexterous Bimanual Manipulation with Coding Agents

## Abstract

> Learning for dexterous manipulation is limited by the scarcity of high-quality demonstrations:
> retargeting from human video is imprecise, teleoperation gives no force feedback to the
> operator, and exoskeleton-based collection is hard to scale up. We present PyRUA-Dex, a
> framework that enables coding agents to develop dexterous manipulation through programmatic
> policy, without the need of demonstrations. The programmatic policy is developed in two stages:
> during exploration, multiple agents explore in parallel for task skills and ways to compose
> them, and a distilling agent condenses the successful attempts into a task library and one
> program. During refinement, this program is improved through randomized layouts for enhanced
> robustness and execution efficiency. In simulated environments, PyRUA-Dex achieves SOTA on
> Bench2Dex with a score of 72.9 and reaches 81.0% success on DexJoCo under full randomization,
> ahead of every trained policy on both benchmarks. In real world experiments on a bimanual
> platform with two 22-DoF hands, PyRUA-Dex learns to play piano, write calligraphy, and other
> tasks efficiently: learning a new piece from sheet music takes as little as 30 minutes. These
> results suggest that PyRUA-Dex is an effective framework for developing dexterous manipulation
> policies with coding agents in the absence of high quality dexterous manipulation
> demonstrations.
