# Schrödinger's Legacy: The Wave Function and Quantum Reality

## Abstract

> The article discusses how Schrödinger arrived at the formulation of wave mechanics and his
> legacy on the philosophy of quantum mechanics, focusing in particular on the metaphysics of the
> wave function. Schrödinger's interpretation of the wave function as electron charge density is
> generally dismissed or presented only as a curious historical example in the literature. On a
> closer look, however, while this interpretation was eventually abandoned in favor of Born's
> statistical interpretation, Schrödinger's idea survived in modern realist approaches to the wave
> function, such as the mass-density approach and the multi-field interpretation. It also proved
> to be of great utility in modern techniques and approximations in quantum chemistry to compute
> the shape of atoms and molecules. Overall, Schrödinger's interpretation of the wave function and
> his realist understanding of quantum mechanics paved the way to modern attempts to define a
> clear ontology of the microscopic world, a tradition that survived the Copenhagen interpretation
> of Bohr and Heisenberg and that is still present today.
