# Electric field control of anisotropic spin traffic between spin-filtering and magnon-scattering in a layered antiferromagnet

## Abstract

> Layered A-type antiferromagnetic (AFM) semiconductors offer a unique platform for spintronic
> devices by combining robust magnetism with gate-tunable semiconducting properties. We present a
> dual-gate field-effect transistor based on CrSBr, a layered A-type AFM with pronounced transport
> anisotropy and complex spin dynamics. Our device provides independent control over carrier
> density and displacement field. Systematic transport measurements reveal that the displacement
> field, rather than doping, predominantly governs the sign and magnitude of magnetoresistance
> ratio (MRR). We identify the origins of both negative and positive MRR components and connect
> them to interlayer and intralayer spin-dependent transport mechanisms. We interpret these
> findings with ab initio calculations and an effective spin-circuit model that reproduces the
> anisotropic magnetotransport. Our results identify the displacement field as an efficient
> electrical control parameter of spin transport in CrSBr and suggest that multi-gate
> architectures could provide similar control in other layered antiferromagnetic semiconductors.
