# Slow mixing of Gibbs sampling for the quantum Curie-Weiss model

## Abstract

> Hamiltonian evolution can spread local operators across a dense quantum magnet. We show that
> this global support does not remove the bottleneck to thermalization in the quantum Curie-Weiss
> model: at fixed low temperature and weak transverse field, the Chen-Kastoryano-Gilyen Gibbs
> sampler [arXiv: 2311.09207] has an exponentially large mixing-time lower bound. A dynamical
> quantum bottleneck theory that generalizes the classical notion of conductance has been proposed
> alongside existing frameworks that rely on spatial locality. We also give a complementary
> interpretation of slow mixing using an effective Fokker-Planck equation.
