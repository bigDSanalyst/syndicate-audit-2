# The Optimal Competitive Ratio for Subadditive Online Combinatorial Auctions is Less Than 1/2

## Abstract

> We construct a four-buyer online combinatorial auction with independent subadditive valuations
> and a fixed, known arrival order in which every online rule earns strictly less than $1/2$ of
> the expected offline optimum. A common partition, revealed at the third arrival, gives the first
> three buyers one unit of welfare per item. If the rule uses three distinct partitions, either an
> item allocated early contributes zero or the fourth buyer's expected value for the remaining
> bundle is one less than its size. We compute the optimal online and offline welfare exactly and
> show that a $1/2$ guarantee always holds with at most three buyers.
