# Ultracold polar molecules for quantum science and technology

## Abstract

> Ultracold polar molecules present a rich platform for experimental quantum science leveraging
> their unique properties. Of particular relevance is the intrinsic electric dipole moment of the
> molecule, which results in strong coupling to microwave fields and can be used to engineer long-
> range, anisotropic, and tunable dipole-dipole interactions. Such interactions can generate
> entanglement between molecules and enable the study of novel quantum many-body phenomena in
> parameter regimes inaccessible in other systems. Modern experiments in optical tweezers and
> optical lattices have demonstrated controlled interactions between molecules in coherent quantum
> states, and recently bulk gases of molecules have been cooled to quantum degeneracy. Motivated
> by these landmark advances, we present this article to explain many of the key concepts relevant
> to current research in the field.
