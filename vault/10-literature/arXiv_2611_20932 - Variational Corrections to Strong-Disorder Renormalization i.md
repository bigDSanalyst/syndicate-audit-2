# Variational Corrections to Strong-Disorder Renormalization in the Two-Dimensional Random Transverse Field Ising Model on Quantum Hardware

## Abstract

> Strong-disorder renormalization-group (SDRG) methods provide an effective description of
> disordered quantum many-body systems by identifying the dominant couplings for the low energy
> physics. However, the resulting renormalization-group flow is approximate and neglects
> correlations beyond the RG description. Here, we investigate whether variational quantum
> optimization can be used to correct these approximations. Building on the emergent-coupling-
> based ansatz (ECBA) developed for the one-dimensional random Heisenberg model, we introduce a
> formulation adapted to the two-dimensional random transverse field Ising model. The SDRG flow
> determines the structure of the circuit, while variational optimization allows the state to
> deviate from the RG approximation. We study the resulting ansatz numerically and experimentally
> on an IQM superconducting quantum processor. For a 16-qubit system, we perform full variational
> optimization on the quantum processor, while a 49-qubit system is investigated using pre-
> optimized parameters, achieving a relative energy accuracy of 89% and 97%, respectively. Our
> results demonstrate that RG information can be translated into shallow, hardware-compatible
> quantum circuits while variational optimization enables corrections beyond the underlying RG
> approximation also for two-dimensional systems.
