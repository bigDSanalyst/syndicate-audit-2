# Probing fluctuations in photon emission statistics of spin injected quantum dots with time dependent electronic excitation

## Abstract

> Electrically spin-injected ensembles of InGaAs/GaAs quantum dots emit circularly polarized
> single photons, with the emission helicity determined by the spin polarization of the injected
> electrons. A ferromagnetic CoFeB spin-injection layer with perpendicular magnetic anisotropy,
> combined with an MgO tunnel barrier, enables spin-polarized electron injection into the quantum-
> dot active region, resulting in circularly polarized photon emission at zero applied magnetic
> field. These devices realize direct spin-charge-photon conversion in an electrically driven,
> microelectronics-compatible quantum light source that operates without an external pump laser.
> Using an extended Hanbury Brown-Twiss interferometer with four detectors and time-tagged
> readout, we measure time-resolved photon-number statistics that resolve up to $|2\rangle$ Fock
> states within a single excitation cycle. While single-photon ($n=|1\rangle$) emission tracks the
> electrical excitation pulse as expected, the two-photon ($n=|2\rangle$) statistics exhibit
> pronounced fluctuations at multiple temporal positions within the pulse. These fluctuations
> vanish upon demagnetization of the spin-injection layer, establishing a direct link between the
> multi-photon emission dynamics and the spin polarization of the injected carriers. The
> observations indicate that spin injection controls not only the helicity of the emitted photons
> but also higher-order temporal correlations of the emission. The behavior is phenomenologically
> captured by inserting both a variable excitation mechanism and a set of multi-emitter collapse
> operators within a stochastic wave function evolution. The platform thus opens a route to
> electrically driven, spin-controlled single and multi-photon Fock-state sources and provides a
> testbed for spin-conditioned multi-emitter dynamics in solid-state quantum light sources.
