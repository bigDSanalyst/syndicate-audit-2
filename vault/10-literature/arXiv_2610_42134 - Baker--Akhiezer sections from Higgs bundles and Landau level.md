# Baker--Akhiezer sections from Higgs bundles and Landau levels in hyperbolic band theory

## Abstract

> As observed by Kienzle and the second author, rank-two $\mathcal{O}(3)$-twisted Higgs bundles on
> the projective line encode all hyperbolic crystals of genus two: the spectral curves are the
> unit cells, and the Hitchin fibers are the abelian Brillouin zones. In a uniform magnetic field
> of two flux quanta, the lowest Landau level is one-dimensional at every nonzero momentum, and,
> as found by Iengo and Li, its dimension jumps to two at the zero momentum. We identify the
> states of the lowest Landau level with the Baker--Akhiezer sections of the Higgs bundles, and
> their zeros, the vortices, with the Baker--Akhiezer divisors. A family of nonzero momenta limits
> to the zero momentum exactly when each of the two vortices approaches the image of the other
> under the spatial inversion, and the states of the jumped level are labeled by the directions of
> limiting. On the Higgs bundle side, the zero momentum is the point where the Hitchin fiber
> intersects the Hitchin section. Such a family of Higgs bundles limits to the Hitchin section,
> thereby crossing to a lower stratum of the \BB stratification, an example of the phenomenon
> investigated in terms of Baker--Akhiezer divisors by the first author with Figiel and Teschner.
