# Mutual Linearity in Nonlinear Population Dynamics

## Abstract

> Mutual Linearity in Nonlinear Population DynamicsMutual linearity, the persistence of affine
> relations among stationary observables under arbitrarily strong changes of one transition rate,
> is known for Markov jump processes and chemical reaction networks. We show that generalized
> Lotka--Volterra dynamics form a new class of mutually linear systems, even though species can go
> extinct and attractors can be chaotic. Any change to one species' equation acts on the others as
> one effective press and keeps all abundances affinely related: piecewise across extinctions, and
> as time averages on chaotic attractors.
