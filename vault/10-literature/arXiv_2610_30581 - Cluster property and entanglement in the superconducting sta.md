# Cluster property and entanglement in the superconducting state

## Abstract

> We present the conditions for satisfying the cluster property in the superconducting state.
> Using these conditions, we show that the cluster property employed when deriving the order
> parameter of the superconducting state (OPSS) holds true, and that the OPSS is given by
> ${}_S\langle Ψ| \hatψ(x)\hatψ(x') | Ψ\rangle_S$, where $|Ψ\rangle_S$ is the superconducting
> state, and where $\hatψ(x)$ is the field operator of superconducting electrons. This provides a
> resolution to the long-standing concern raised by Leggett et al.\ regarding particle-number
> fluctuation in the superconducting state. In addition, by considering the entanglement between
> two particles composed of the maximum geminal of the BCS ground state [J.\ Phys. Commun. 5,
> 095003 (2021)], it is confirmed that the larger the deviation from the conditions for satisfying
> the cluster property becomes, the greater the magnitude of the entanglement becomes. This shows
> not only the effectiveness of the conditions for satisfying the cluster property but also the
> justification of the OPSS that has been commonly used.
