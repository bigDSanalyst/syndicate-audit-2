# Jianmu-Q: Bridging Quantum Field Theory and Quantum Hardware with AI Agents

## Abstract

> Quantum computing offers a promising approach to non-perturbative simulations of quantum field
> theories, particularly for real-time dynamics and systems affected by the sign problem. Yet
> translating continuum field theories into quantum computations remains a challenging
> interdisciplinary task. Here we introduce \texttt{Jianmu-Q}, a multi-agent framework that
> transforms natural-language descriptions of quantum field theories into quantum simulation
> workflows. Specialized agents handle theoretical formulation, lattice discretization, qubit
> mapping, and circuit design, while physical consistency checks and independent numerical
> calculations provide validation throughout the workflow. We benchmark the framework on three
> field theories of increasing complexity: the $(1+1)$-dimensional Nambu--Jona-Lasinio and
> Schwinger models, and a $(2+1)$-dimensional $\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge theory. Across these systems,
> noiseless quantum-circuit simulations converge toward independent exact-diagonalization and
> tensor-network results, while selected circuits are executed on IBM quantum hardware,
> demonstrating an end-to-end, validation-guided approach to quantum simulation of field theories.
