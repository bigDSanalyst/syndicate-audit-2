# Relativistic Strong Quantum Mpemba Effect

## Abstract

> The Unruh effect links acceleration to thermalization, providing a relativistic setting for
> anomalous quantum relaxation. We demonstrate a {\it relativistic} strong quantum Mpemba effect
> in the Unruh thermalization of a two-level detector coupled to a massless scalar field. For a
> fixed pair of isospectral initial states, relaxation curves that never cross in inertial motion
> exhibit a Mpemba crossing above a threshold acceleration. At a higher, precisely tuned
> acceleration, the initially farther state has zero overlap with the slowest decay mode and
> relaxes with a faster asymptotic exponential rate. We identify how competition between
> transition and dephasing channels governs the relaxation spectrum, where sufficiently strong
> longitudinal coupling enables acceleration to switch the relaxation bottleneck between
> population and coherence modes. We derive the spectral-switching condition and construct
> isospectral preparation protocols that eliminate slow-mode contributions within their physical
> domains. These protocols reveal how coherence can suppress or enhance slow-mode overlap at fixed
> purity, depending on the underlying spectral structure. Our results establish acceleration as a
> control parameter for anomalous quantum relaxation, connecting relativistic quantum dynamics
> with nonequilibrium thermodynamics.
