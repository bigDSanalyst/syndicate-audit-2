# Entanglement certification of attosecond light fields with photoelectron interferometry

## Abstract

> The emergence of entangled quantum optical states in high-harmonic generation raises a
> fundamental problem about its characterization, namely, \textit{how can entanglement of extreme-
> ultraviolet radiation be certified for attosecond light fields?} Here, we provide experimentally
> accessible separability tests, particularly showing that attosecond photoelectron interferometry
> provides a direct solution to this problem. This is achieved by a quantum optical treatment of
> mapping photoelectron interferometry to the measurement of optical correlation functions. This
> yields a negative-partial-transpose witness for infrared-XUV entanglement and a sufficient
> witness of genuine multipartite entanglement without auxiliary optical measurements. We evaluate
> both witnesses on explicit optical states and identify how the protocol can be extended to XUV-
> XUV entanglement certification. Hence, we establish attosecond photoelectron interferometry as a
> general framework for entanglement certification across a wide range of optical frequencies.
