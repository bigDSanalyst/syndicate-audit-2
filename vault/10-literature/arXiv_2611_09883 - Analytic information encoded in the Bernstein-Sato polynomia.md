# Analytic information encoded in the Bernstein-Sato polynomial of genus-one plane branches

## Abstract

> We give an explicit formula recovering the Zariski invariant of a plane branch with one Puiseux
> pair from its Bernstein-Sato polynomial. Thus the Bernstein-Sato stratification refines the
> Zariski-invariant stratification. We determine the generic roots in each stratum from the value
> semigroup and the Zariski invariant, refining Yano's description for a fixed topological class.
> We also compute the maximal spectral-exponent discrepancy and relate it to the dimension of the
> generic Bernstein-Sato locus and the minimum Tjurina number.
