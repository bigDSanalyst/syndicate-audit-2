# On the pure part of the cohomology of character varieties

## Abstract

> We prove the purity conjecture of Hausel and Rodriguez-Villegas relating the graded dimension of
> the pure part of the cohomology of the twisted character variety $M^B(r,d)$ of $GL(r)$ of genus
> $g$ with the cohomology of a suitable quiver variety associated to the quiver $S_g$ with one
> vertex and $g$ loops. In particular, this provides an explicit formula for the pure part of the
> mixed Hodge polynomial of $M^B(r,d)$ in terms of the Kac polynomial $A_{S_g,r}(q)$. Our approach
> is based on the $P=W$ theorem to transfer the purity conjecture into a statement about the
> perverse filtration on the cohomology of th stack of Higgs bundles on a genus $g$ curve. We use
> the theory of BPS Lie algebras, their $χ$-independence property (proven by Kinjo-Koseki), the
> recent proof of the stack version of $P=C$ due to Picovschi and Davison's analytic neighborhood
> theorem to reduce the conjecture to a comparison problem between (the 'pure' part) of the BPS
> Lie algebra asociated to the stack of Higgs bundles on a genus $g$ curve and the BPS Lie algebra
> of the stack of representations of the preprojective algebra of the quiver $S_g$. This last step
> is taken care of by a quiver analog of Markman's tautological generation theorem for the BPS Lie
> algebra of arbitrary quivers (this last result is also proven by Davison and Hennecart).
