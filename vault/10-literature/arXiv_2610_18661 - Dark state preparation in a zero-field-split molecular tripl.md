# Dark state preparation in a zero-field-split molecular triplet

## Abstract

> We propose a protocol to prepare and probe dark states in a single-molecule electron spin
> resonance based on atomic force microscopy (ESR-AFM), thus extending existing techniques
> developed to address Rabi oscillations. To this end, we simulate in analogy to atomic physics
> the coherent manipulation of a three-level system in the V-configuration using a multifrequency
> magnetic field. More specifically, we consider a neutral molecule with a zero-field-split (ZFS)
> first excited triplet and analyze within a pump-probe scheme the dynamics of the total triplet
> population. In the triplet subspace, the dark state is a coherent superposition formed by two
> ZFS states which does not evolve in time despite a continuous resonant driving towards the third
> one. We characterize the properties of this state comparing it to its fully incoherent
> counterpart. Within a density-matrix formalism for open systems, we then compute the expected
> dark state signatures both for ideal scenarios and for the realistic parameterization of ESR-AFM
> on pentacene.
