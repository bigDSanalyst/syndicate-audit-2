# A stable multipersistence invariant for ladder modules

## Abstract

> We define an additive functor S that assigns a three parameter persistence module to every
> morphism between one parameter persistence modules. We show that if the morphism is bar-to-bar,
> then its image under S is rectangle decomposable. We prove that S is stable with respect to the
> interleaving distance and study further properties of the functor related to rectangle
> decompositions.
