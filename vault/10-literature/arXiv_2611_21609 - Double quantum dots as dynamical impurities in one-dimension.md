# Double quantum dots as dynamical impurities in one-dimensional transport

## Abstract

> We study single-electron scattering in a tight-binding chain capacitively coupled to double
> quantum dots, which act as dynamical two-level-system impurities with electrically tunable level
> splittings. For a single impurity, we solve the scattering problem exactly and show Feshbach-
> like perfect elastic reflection, whose resonant condition distinguishes the initial two-level
> state. For several impurities in a strongly reflecting chain, transmission peaks at an
> intermediate mean level splitting. At small splittings, energy exchange with the impurities can
> weaken constructive interference between backscattered paths, consistent with the initial rise
> in transmission. As excitation channels close, virtual transitions can enhance reflection and
> drive transmission below its purely elastic value $T_0$. At larger splittings, these virtual
> processes weaken and transmission approaches $T_0$. We discuss how gate-controlled transmission
> measurements in quantum-dot arrays could test these predictions.
