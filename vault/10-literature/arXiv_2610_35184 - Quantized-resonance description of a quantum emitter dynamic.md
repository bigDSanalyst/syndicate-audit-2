# Quantized-resonance description of a quantum emitter dynamics in a lossy multimode cavity

## Abstract

> In the multimode regime of cavity quantum electrodynamics (cQED), light-matter coupling can be
> strong enough such as to allows a quantum emitter to couple with multiple resonant field modes.
> This regime leaves distinctive signatures in the behavior of both the field and the emitter,
> which are already accessible by modern experimental techniques. To model such effects in lossy
> cavities, effective discrete-mode approaches have been widely employed, despite their accuracy
> beyond two modes remaining rarely benchmarked. In this work, we demonstrate the necessity of
> employing multiple modes to describe the multimode signatures by analyzing the dynamics of an
> atom in a one-dimensional lossy resonator. The atom's dynamics is simulated using the formalism
> developed in Weitzel et al. (2026) (arXiv:2512.18478), where a resonance quantization
> prescription is provided. We investigate its dependency with respect to two different
> parameters: the light-matter coupling strength and the atom's position. In the former case, we
> find that the minimal number of modes necessary to capture the essential features of the atom's
> dynamics increases with the coupling strength. In the latter, we further observe that the
> multimode regime leads to a strong position-dependency of the atom's dynamics. In all cases, we
> discuss the physical interpretation of our results and further discuss about how the employed
> formalism can be used to recover physically relevant quantities.
