# Universal Quantum Criticality from a Gaussian Variational Manifold

## Abstract

> Universal behavior at continuous phase transitions is encoded in long-wavelength fixed-point
> theories controlled by dimensionality and symmetries. It is a central question for which systems
> this universal behavior and the underlying long-wavelength description can be captured by a
> noninteracting theory allowing for analytical and accurate predictions. In this work we provide
> a systematic procedure for obtaining the best noninteracting fermionic description of a quantum
> phase transition through fermionic Gaussian states. We demonstrate the performance of our
> procedure for the interacting long-range transverse-field Ising chain. This formulation reduces
> the problem to a self-consistent Euler equation that yields a critical line in close agreement
> with independent numerical benchmarks, while its singular momentum structure provides analytic
> predictions for continuously varying critical properties. Yet exact diagonalization shows that
> Gaussian-state fidelity to the exact ground state is poorest, and non-Gaussianity strongest,
> near the transition. Thus, accurate predictions for critical properties can emerge from the
> Gaussian variational description even where its microscopic wave-function approximation is
> weakest.
