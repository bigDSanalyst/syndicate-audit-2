# Non-Markovian exceptional points and information backflow: a Heisenberg reaction-coordinate approach

## Abstract

> The primary pathway to the investigation of exceptional points (EPs) in open quantum systems has
> been the use of Markovian master equations. However, recently it has been realised that they can
> arise in settings that show non-Markovian behaviour. The main approach in this direction has
> been the use of pseudomodes, which allow the influence of a structured environment to be
> replaced by new damped auxiliary modes. In this work, we use a Heisenberg-equation approach
> which relies on the reaction-coordinate formalism to obtain exact analytical dynamics. First, we
> show how our method considerably simplifies the analysis compared to the use of master
> equations. Second, we utilise it to discuss non-Markovian revivals in systems with second and
> higher-order EPs, showing that the notion that EPs demarcate the onset of revivals is not
> generally true. We then utilise our results to discuss the dynamical effects of higher-order
> non-Markovian EPs, deriving bounds on the number of revivals and investigating enhanced
> asymptotic relaxation near higher-order EPs. Finally, we investigate non-Markovian EPs with
> engineered reservoirs and show how they can be utilised to control the rate of irreversible
> information loss.
