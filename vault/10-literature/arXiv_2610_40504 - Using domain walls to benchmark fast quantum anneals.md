# Using domain walls to benchmark fast quantum anneals

## Abstract

> We consider the distribution of domain walls arising in ground states of frustrated
> 1-dimensional Ising spin chains with anti-parallel boundary spins. For ideal chains, a sine-
> squared particle-in-a-box distribution of domain walls is expected in the quantum setting, and a
> uniform distribution expected classically. However, for chains with random imperfections, prior
> work [Chancellor et al., npj Quantum Inf. 8, 73, 2022] found that the distribution in a physical
> implementation corresponded to neither. Instead, the domain wall distribution forms a U-shaped
> profile arising from a classical model with random fields, where the combination of averaging
> over these fields and thermal averaging produces the characteristic U-shape. With D-Wave's newer
> fast anneal feature, we access anneal times shorter than the coherence time and observe the
> expected coherent quantum behavior in the form of a sine-squared distribution. For the shortest
> available anneal time, the dynamics have not yet reached the sine-squared shape and instead
> display a different U-shape in the middle of the chains. This motivates using the extent of the
> domain wall distribution tail in this regime as a platform independent metric that provides a
> signature of both coherence time and noise levels.
