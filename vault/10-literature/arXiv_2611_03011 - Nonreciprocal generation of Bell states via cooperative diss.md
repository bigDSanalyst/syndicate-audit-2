# Nonreciprocal generation of Bell states via cooperative dissipation in a qubit-magnon system

## Abstract

> Directionally generating and controlling quantum states is critical in chiral quantum
> technologies. Here, we propose a scheme for the nonreciprocal generation of Bell states via
> cooperative dissipation in a qubit-magnon system, consisting of a superconducting transmon qubit
> and a yttrium iron garnet sphere subject to a bias magnetic field. The cooperative dissipation
> between them is induced by coupling both to a common transmission-line waveguide. Due to this
> cooperative dissipation, the system possesses a dark eigenstate within the single-excitation
> subspace. Under appropriate parameter conditions, the dark eigenstate reduces to a specific Bell
> state. With the assistance of a drive pulse, the system can be prepared in this Bell state
> (i.e., the dark eigenstate). In particular, we find that the dark eigenstate corresponds to
> different Bell states under positive ($+\mathbf{B}_0$) and negative ($-\mathbf{B}_0$) bias
> fields. Consequently, nonreciprocal Bell-state generation is achieved: $+\mathbf{B}_0$ yields
> one Bell state, while $-\mathbf{B}_0$ yields the other. Moreover, we define a fidelity-based
> nonreciprocal rate to quantify this nonreciprocity. Our work establishes cooperative dissipation
> as a versatile resource for nonreciprocal entangled-state generation, opening a promising route
> toward chiral quantum technologies.
