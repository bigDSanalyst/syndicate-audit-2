# Scalable Passive QRAM

## Abstract

> We present a blueprint for implementing the physical QRAM operation in a scalable, passive
> manner, proving its feasibility in principle. Our construction is passive because for an
> $n$-qubit address over $N = 2^n$ memory bits the energy cost per QRAM query is $O(\log N)$, and
> to our knowledge it is the first construction to use $\operatorname{polylog} N$ energy. The
> runtime for each query is $O(\log^2 N)$. It is achieved by evolving a pre-manufactured time-
> independent 4-local Hamiltonian with $O(N)$ terms where every qubit participates in at most 4
> terms.
