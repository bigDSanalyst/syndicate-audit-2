# Landau quantization of $C_3$ high-fold fermions in honeycomb lattices

## Abstract

> We present the theory of the Landau levels of $C_3$ high-fold fermions in honeycomb lattices.
> These fermions arise as a generalization of the tight-binding model in a honeycomb lattice with
> one orbital per site, known to describe graphene, to a family of models with several orbitals
> per site transforming according to representations of $C_3$, relevant for triangulene two-
> dimensional crystals. The models are defined by the number of orbitals $n_a$ and $n_b$ at the
> $a$ and $b$ sites of the unit cell. They feature energy bands that combine flat bands and Dirac
> cones, that are isomorphic to those of graphene, the Lieb, $p_{xy}$ honeycomb, and the Kagome
> tight-binding models. Combining tight-binding and $k\cdot p$ methods, here we compute the Landau
> levels of the $(1,1)$, $(1,2)$, $(2,2)$ and $(3,3)$ models, we compare them to those of the
> reference models, and we explore the similarities and differences with the Landau levels in
> graphene. Our findings reveal that the nature of $C_3$ high-fold fermions combine Dirac-type
> Landau quantization, originating from the linear crossings and associated relativistic band
> dispersion, with anomalous Landau quantization rooted in the quantum geometry of the singular
> flat bands.
