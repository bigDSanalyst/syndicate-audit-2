# On the gauge-invariant orbital angular momentum of vector optical vortices in the paraxial approximation and beyond

## Abstract

> In this paper we present a gauge-invariant formalism for describing the spin and orbital angular
> momenta of scalar and vector optical vortices. The correction term, arising from consistent
> incorporation of the transverse vector potential's nonlocality, restores gauge invariance and
> makes it possible to separate the total angular momentum into spin and orbital components. We
> propose a method for calculating the orbital and spin angular momenta of multimode vector vortex
> fields and show that the orbital angular momentum of such beams contains two qualitatively
> different components. The polarization-independent contribution to the orbital angular momentum
> is related to the topological charge that determines the order of the phase singularity, while
> the polarization-dependent one is proportional to the helical topological charge that determines
> the spatial inhomogeneity of the polarization. We also discuss the quantization procedure for
> fields with spatially inhomogeneous polarization in terms of modes with a helical topological
> charge, and examine the limits of applicability of the proposed formalism.
