# Locally infrared finite two-loop amplitudes for multi Higgs production in association with one jet

## Abstract

> We study the two-loop amplitudes for multi-Higgs production through a massive top-quark loop in
> association with one jet. We consider the quark-antiquark channel with a final-state gluon,
> $q\bar q\to g+nH$ although our construction applies to crossed channel as well. We utilize (non-
> local in momentum space) Ward identities to factorize longitudinal and gauge degrees of freedom
> through exact identities between Feynman graphs without reference to an infrared region. In the
> genuine two loop topologies, we replace the internal gluons by their physical degrees of freedom
> and show that the resulting amplitude is free of collinear singularities. The only remaining
> infrared divergence is a soft singularity that can be identified with a change of reference
> gauge vector. We construct explicit soft counterterms for these singularities, obtaining a
> locally infrared-finite representation. We verify that the factorized longitudinal degrees of
> freedom reproduce the remaining infrared poles of the original Feynman graphs. Our formalism
> requires no counter-terms for collinear finiteness or for manifesting Ward identities in various
> regions since we perform a decomposition of covariant gauge Feynman graphs. We expect its
> extension to more complicated processes to eliminate all collinear singularities--particularly
> in the initial state.
