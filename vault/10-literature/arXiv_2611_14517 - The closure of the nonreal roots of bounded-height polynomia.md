# The closure of the nonreal roots of bounded-height polynomials is connected exactly at pronic heights

## Abstract

> Let C_h be the closure of the nonreal roots of integer polynomials with coefficients of modulus
> at most h. With the nonzero real roots included, the closure is connected at every height;
> without them, connectedness becomes arithmetic. We prove that C_h is connected exactly when
> h=q(q-1) is a pronic number 2, 6, 12, 20, ..., and has at least five components otherwise. At
> height two this confirms a twenty-year-old numerical observation of Shmerkin and Solomyak on
> double zeros of power series: apart from 1/2 they form the interval $[1/ρ,1)=C_2\cap(0,1)$, with
> $ρ$ the tribonacci constant. Disconnection is almost always infinite. For (p-1)^2<h<=p^2-3,
> $h\neq p(p-1)$, infinitely many components with nonempty interior accumulate at $\pm p$ and $\pm
> 1/p$, and when h<p(p-1) these four points are themselves components. Next to each square
> q^2>=16, at least two of the three heights q^2-2, q^2-1, q^2 have infinitely many components
> accumulating at a common point of (1/(q+1),1/q). Explicit series give infinitely many components
> at every nonpronic height below 167 other than 1, 3, 4, 9, 25, 63 and 81, and in general at most
> sqrt(X)+1 nonpronic heights h<=X can have finitely many components, although C_h is a Peano
> compactum at every height. The proofs compare values and derivatives of all admissible power
> series near a real double zero; the comparison sets are open and closed in the whole core, so
> components separated near the axis cannot reconnect elsewhere. On the real axis, the double
> zeros at heights 1, 3, 4, 7 form sets with infinitely many components; at height one this proves
> a conjecture of Shmerkin and Solomyak and one of Solomyak on the connectedness locus for pairs
> of affine maps.
