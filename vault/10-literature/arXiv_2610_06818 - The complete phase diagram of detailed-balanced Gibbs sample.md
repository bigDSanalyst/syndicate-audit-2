# The complete phase diagram of detailed-balanced Gibbs samplers for the mean-field Heisenberg model

## Abstract

> We study the spectral gap of recently discovered families of KMS-detailed-balanced Gibbs-sampler
> Lindbladians for the mean-field Heisenberg model on $n$ spins. We show that the spectral gaps of
> these natural families of Lindbladians admit the same phase diagram: the gap is constant above
> the critical temperature (i.e., in the disordered phase), scales as $1/\sqrt{n}$ at the critical
> temperature, and as $1/n$ below the critical temperature (i.e., in the ordered phase). Our
> result builds on and extends the recent work of Basso et al.~(2026) which characterized this
> phase diagram for the GNS-detailed-balanced Davies generator, but only partially resolved the
> gap scaling at the critical temperature.
