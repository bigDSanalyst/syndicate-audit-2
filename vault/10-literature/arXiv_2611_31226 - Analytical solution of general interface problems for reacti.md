# Analytical solution of general interface problems for reaction-advection-diffusion equations

## Abstract

> We obtain novel integral representations, expressed as contour integrals in the complex Fourier
> plane, for the solution of fully nonhomogeneous interface problems for classical reaction-
> advection-diffusion (RAD) equations with arbitrary initial data on the line and general
> interface conditions at the origin. Such problems of applied mathematics emerge in a spectacular
> variety of physical phenomena in mathematical physics, chemistry, continuum mechanics, biology,
> pharmacokinetics, as well as mathematical finance. A suitable implementation of Fokas' unified
> transform method is in force herein, with particular challenges arising due to inclusion in the
> model of lower-order terms, and the generality of interface problems under consideration. To our
> knowledge, this careful analysis has not appeared in (nor does it follow easily from) literature
> published so far. Our explicit formulae directly lend themselves to explorations of solutions'
> qualitative properties such as regularity, asymptotic behavior and well-posedness. This work has
> also been of utility in the investigation of nonlinear counterparts as well as in the study of
> phase-transition phenomena, moving-interface and diffuse-interface problems. Such results for
> RAD and high-order PDEs will soon appear in a series of separate papers.
