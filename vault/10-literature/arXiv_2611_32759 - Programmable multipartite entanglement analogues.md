# Programmable multipartite entanglement analogues

## Abstract

> Quantum entanglement underpins the quantum information science. Its multipartite forms
> distribute coherence across several subsystems, but controlling their composition and order is
> increasingly difficult as more subsystems participate. Classical nonseparable waves provide
> accessible analogues of these structures, although existing implementations largely fix the
> participating degrees of freedom through their generating architectures. Here we overcome this
> constraint by making multipartiteness itself programmable in electromagnetic fields. A
> spatiotemporal metasurface reconfigures the identity and number of coherently linked state
> spaces independently across polarization, frequency, orbital angular momentum, wavevector
> direction, and orthogonal envelope modes. We realize complete Bell-like and Greenberger-Horne-
> Zeilinger-like families from bipartite to five-partite order and exchange constituents at fixed
> order. To our knowledge, the five-partite state has the highest multipartite order
> experimentally realized in classical electromagnetic fields. These results establish the
> distribution of coherence across physical state spaces as a programmable property of classical
> waves and provide a platform for studying selected structures of multipartite quantum states
> without invoking quantum entanglement or nonlocality.
