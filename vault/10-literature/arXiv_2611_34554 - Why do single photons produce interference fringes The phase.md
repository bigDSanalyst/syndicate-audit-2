# Why do single photons produce interference fringes? The phase of a mode and the phase of a state

## Abstract

> If the phase of a photon-number state is completely undetermined, how can single photons, sent
> one at a time through a two-slit apparatus, build up interference fringes? These lecture notes
> answer this question from first principles. Starting from Maxwell's equations, we expand the
> field in vector modes, quantize it, and introduce the quantity measured by a photodetector. We
> then show that the word ``phase'' refers to two different things: the phase of a mode, fixed by
> the optical setup, and the phase of the quantum state that excites the mode. Only the first
> determines the fringes. All calculations are given in full, and problems with solutions are
> included.
