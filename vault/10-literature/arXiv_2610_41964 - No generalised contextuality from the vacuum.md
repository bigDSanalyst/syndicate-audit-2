# No generalised contextuality from the vacuum

## Abstract

> We show that, under certain conditions, generalised contextuality, in the sense introduced by
> Spekkens, cannot be created from the vacuum of a quantum field. Initial noncontextual qudit
> states do not become contextual just by interacting with a quantum field. This is verified in
> two scenarios. First, in a perturbative approach where the time evolution operator is expanded
> in powers of the coupling constant. Second, in the limit of zero energy gap in the spectrum of
> the free detector Hamiltonian. Our results are at the opposite side of what has been recently
> obtained in the literature regarding the harvesting of Kochen-Specker contextuality. Our results
> demonstrate that the existing notions of contextuality can be independent of one another and
> that contextuality harvesting depends on which precise notion is used.
