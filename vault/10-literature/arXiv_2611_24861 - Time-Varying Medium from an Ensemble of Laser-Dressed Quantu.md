# Time-Varying Medium from an Ensemble of Laser-Dressed Quantum Emitters

## Abstract

> We propose a minimal quantum-mechanical model of a frequency dispersive, time-modulated medium
> that explicitly incorporates the pumping mechanism. It consists of a homogeneous ensemble of
> quantum emitters dressed by a pump laser. We analyze their dispersive, time-varying response to
> a perturbative probe field by deriving the macroscopic polarizability of the ensemble, and then
> solving Maxwell's equations via the Floquet formalism. This yields a rich band structure that
> departs from conventional analyses in which the modulation source is neglected, arising from the
> interplay between the dispersive polaritonic and time-varying responses. These results shed
> light on the role of the modulation source in time-varying media and pave the way toward
> realizing dispersive time-varying media from driven quantum-mechanical systems.
