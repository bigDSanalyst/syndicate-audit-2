# Quantum Metrology and Quantum Battery in a Hyperfine-Coupled Spin System

## Abstract

> We investigate multiparameter quantum metrology and the energetic properties of an effective
> electron--proton spin system inspired by the hyperfine structure of the hydrogen atom.
> Considering the external magnetic field $\mathcal B$ and the hyperfine coupling strength $Λ$ as
> unknown parameters, we derive the quantum Fisher information matrix and establish the
> corresponding quantum Cramér--Rao bounds for their simultaneous estimation. We analyze the
> estimation precision and the correlations between the two parameters, showing that the weak-
> commutativity condition is satisfied despite the noncommutativity of the associated symmetric
> logarithmic derivatives. Independently, we explore the energetic properties of the system under
> a unitary charging protocol, characterizing its ergotropy, anti-ergotropy, storage capacity, and
> energy-basis coherence. We investigate the dependence of these quantities on the magnetic field,
> hyperfine coupling, temperature, and charging time. Our results provide a comprehensive
> characterization of the metrological properties and energetic performance of a hyperfine spin-
> based quantum system, highlighting its potential as a platform for quantum sensing and energy
> storage.
