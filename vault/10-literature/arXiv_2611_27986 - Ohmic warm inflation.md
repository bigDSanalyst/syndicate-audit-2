# Ohmic warm inflation

## Abstract

> We show that Warm Little Inflation is anomalous, lacks a suitable inflaton potential, and cannot
> sustain the thermal bath, as the quasi-static approximation over-estimated the dissipated power.
> To address these issues, we propose a new friction mechanism and new anomaly-free models based
> on one fermion charged under a U(1) gauge group in one extra dimension. The four-dimensional
> lattice version is a ring of $N$ U(1) groups with one fermion at each site, reducing to an
> anomaly-free version of the warm little inflaton model for $N=2$. The inflaton is a Wilson line,
> protected for $N\ge5$. Large $N$ allows a suitable monodromy inflaton potential. In the
> continuum limit, $N\to\infty$, the inflaton velocity becomes a five-dimensional electric field
> and friction becomes Ohmic dissipation, which seems able to sustain warm inflation compatibly
> with observations.
