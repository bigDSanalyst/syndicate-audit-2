# A Fractional Chern Semimetal: Exact Wavefunction and Conformal Criticality

## Abstract

> Unlike fractional quantum Hall states in uniform Landau levels, fractional Chern insulators can
> host dispersive anyon bands, raising the question of what happens when the anyon gap closes. We
> construct an exact finite-bond-dimension tensor-network state for a fractional Chern semimetal
> by Gutzwiller projecting a critical parton state. This state exhibits divergent correlation
> lengths in charged sectors but a fractionally quantized many-body Chern number of $1/3$.
> Although it has one electron per unit cell, its modular $S$ and $T$ matrices and entanglement
> spectrum match those of the $ν=1/3$ Laughlin state. We identify a $(1+1)$--dimensional
> $U(3)_1\times SU(3)_1$ conformal field theory (CFT) governing the wavefunction norm in the
> infrared, an unexpected result for a state with $U(1)_3$ topology. The $SU(3)_1$ sector is a
> nonlinear sigma model whose Wess--Zumino level and kinetic stiffness are determined by the
> parton Chern number and Fubini--Study metric, respectively. Under renormalization, it flows to
> the conformal fixed point, with an initial distance to this fixed point controlled by the
> idealness of parton quantum geometry. Favorable geometry enables precise agreement with CFT
> predictions even on narrow cylinders.
