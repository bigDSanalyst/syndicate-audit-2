# Heisenberg-Scaling Quantum Metrology of Electron-Photon Coupling via Multi-Electron PINEM

## Abstract

> Free-electron quantum optics has recently emerged as a promising platform for coherent electron-
> light interactions, yet its potential for quantum-enhanced metrology remains largely unexplored.
> Here, we investigate the fundamental precision limit of coupling-strength estimation in multi-
> electron photon-induced near-field electron microscopy (PINEM), demonstrating that the quantum
> Fisher information scales quadratically with electron number. We then propose an experimentally
> accessible scheme combining squeezed-light probing and optical homodyne detection that attains
> Heisenberg scaling with respect to electron number, provided the electrons exhibit nonzero
> inter-electron coherence, while surpassing conventional PINEM sensing in photon-number
> enhancement as well. We further analyze the effect of imperfect electron preparation, showing
> that electron phase noise is converted into a fluctuating optical displacement and eventually
> limits the precision. To overcome this limitation, an auxiliary optical mode coupled to the same
> electron ensemble measures the displacement noise in the signal mode, allowing its conditional
> suppression and recovery of the Heisenberg scaling. Our work establishes multi-electron
> coherence as a resource for free-electron quantum metrology and opens the route to precision
> measurements of nanoscale electron-light interactions.
