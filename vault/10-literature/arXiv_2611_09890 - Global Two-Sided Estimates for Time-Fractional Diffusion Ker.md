# Global Two-Sided Estimates for Time-Fractional Diffusion Kernels on Hyperbolic Space

## Abstract

> We study time-fractional diffusion on real hyperbolic space $\mathbb H^n$, $n\ge2$, with Caputo
> order $0<α<1$. We establish global two-sided pointwise estimates for the homogeneous fundamental
> kernel $Z_{\mathbb H}(t,r)$ and the inhomogeneous (Duhamel) kernel $Y_{\mathbb H}(t,r)$,
> uniformly over all $t>0$ and positive geodesic distances $r$. By combining inverse-stable
> subordination with global bounds for the hyperbolic heat kernel, we distinguish a short-time
> local regime, a large-time interior region, and an exterior region governed by a saddle-point
> problem with parameter $r/t$. Both kernels have the same exterior exponential rate, while their
> amplitudes are related by an explicit saddle-point factor. The estimates are uniformly
> comparable on the interfaces between regimes, recover the Euclidean time-fractional
> singularities at small scales, and identify the influence of hyperbolic geometry on large-scale
> decay. The kernel $Z_{\mathbb H}$ also describes the one-time marginal density of hyperbolic
> Brownian motion under an independent inverse stable clock. This representation yields a radial
> limit law, moment and quantile asymptotics, and a change in the mean square displacement from
> order $t^α$ at small times to order $t^{2α}$ at large times.
