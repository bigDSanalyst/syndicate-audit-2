# Constructive Arboricity in Almost-Linear Time for Dense Graphs

## Abstract

> The arboricity of a graph is the minimum number of forests needed to partition its edges. We
> present a randomized algorithm that computes the exact arboricity of a simple graph and
> constructs an optimal forest partition in $\min\{n^{2+o(1)},m^{4/3+o(1)}\}$ time both in
> expectation and with high probability, where $n$ and $m$ denote the numbers of vertices and
> edges. In particular, it runs in almost-linear time, $O(m^{1+o(1)})$, on dense graphs with
> $m=Θ(n^2)$. We also give the first known almost-linear-time algorithm that constructs a
> partition into at most one more forest than optimal.
