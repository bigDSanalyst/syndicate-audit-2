# Raman anyon-Hubbard model in a 1D Bose lattice gas

## Abstract

> The anyon-Hubbard model is the paradigmatic lattice model of one-dimensional (1D) anyons, in
> which chiral interactions make bosons equivalent to particles with fractional statistics. Its
> continuum limit, the chiral BF theory, emerges as the low-energy description of a Raman-coupled
> Bose gas with imbalanced interactions. Here, we show that loading such a gas into a 1D optical
> lattice realizes the lattice counterpart of this theory, which we name the Raman anyon-Hubbard
> model. The Raman-dressed bosons acquire density-dependent Peierls phases when tunneling through
> occupied sites. For a small statistical phase, the model reduces to the anyon-Hubbard model,
> while beyond this limit it defines a distinct lattice anyon model with unbounded rather than
> periodic statistical interactions. Numerically, we show that the dressed gas displays the
> defining properties of 1D anyons, namely a density-dependent asymmetry of the ground-state
> momentum distribution, interaction-induced asymmetric transport and chiral bound states.
> Remarkably, these features are readily accessible with Raman-coupled $^{39}$K atoms, opening up
> the experimental exploration of 1D anyons beyond the few-particle regime.
