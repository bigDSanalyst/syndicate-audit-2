# Invariance of log plurigenera in families of compact Kähler manifolds

## Abstract

> Let $(X_t,B_t)$ be a smooth proper family of pairs with Kähler fibres, where the boundary has
> relative simple normal crossings and real coefficients in $[0,1)$. We show that for every
> $m\ge1$, every section of $mK_{X_t}+\lfloor mB_t\rfloor$ extends locally over the base. In
> particular, the log plurigenera are locally constant, and for $B_t=0$ we obtain the deformation
> invariance of the plurigenera $P_m(X_t)$. Since the total space need not be Kähler, we run the
> minimal model program on the central fibre, using the work of Hacon-Wang and Hacon-Xie, and
> extend its steps over a disc. The obstruction to lifting a jet vanishes along the fixed part
> determined by the final model, and so it is square-integrable for the weight in the extension
> theorem of Cao-Păun. As an application, if the adjoint is pseudoeffective on one fibre, then the
> pair has a relative good minimal model near each point of the base and a relative canonical
> model over the whole base.
