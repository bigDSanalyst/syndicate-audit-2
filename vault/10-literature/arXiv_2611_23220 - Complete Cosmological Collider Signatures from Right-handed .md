# Complete Cosmological Collider Signatures from Right-handed Neutrino Loops

## Abstract

> We study the complete cosmological collider (CC) signatures from right-handed neutrino loops
> with chemical-potential enhancement. These CC signals are induced by the inflaton-right-handed-
> neutrino interaction and can be extracted in the collinear squeezed configuration, which allows
> the right-handed-neutrino triangle loop to be factorized into the hard and soft parts. We
> precisely compute the soft part of the fermion loop for the first time by using the complete
> mode functions and the full loop integral, and treat the hard part as a tree-level subdiagram.
> We subtract the large-momentum asymptotic terms of the soft loop integrand and retain their
> finite contributions to the oscillation. We then evaluate the full spinor-helicity and
> Schwinger-Keldysh sums to obtain a finite, closed analytic formula for the complex clock
> coefficient. We further derive a compact formula for the leading CC signals in the large-
> chemical-potential expansion. We analyze the structure of the soft-part contribution by
> examining the sum of residues of the Mellin poles of the fermion propagators. For this, we
> demonstrate that the first local pole on each soft line contributes half of the asymptotic
> coefficient on one Schwinger-Keldysh path and the remaining local poles on the second soft line
> supply the other half.
