# Finite-width altermagnetic quantum rings: angular-channel hybridization and Aharonov--Bohm physics

## Abstract

> We study a finite-width two-dimensional quantum ring with a quadratic $d$-wave altermagnetic
> term, Tan--Inkson confinement, a perpendicular magnetic field, and independently tunable
> Aharonov--Bohm (AB) flux. The altermagnetic interaction mixes $m$ and $m\pm2$ angular channels
> while preserving their parity. Circular symmetry renders the crystal orientation spectrally
> irrelevant and makes the two spin blocks exactly isospectral. The resulting same-parity
> anticrossings are resolved at low filling, and multichannel mixing is found near the Fermi level
> of highly occupied rings. Independent converged calculations for $N_e=1400$ and $1800$ establish
> the reconstructed Fermi edge and magnetic response in a finite field window and the persistent
> current over a full AB flux period. The analytic conventional reference separates radial-subband
> depopulation (dHvA-type cycles) from finer finite-ring shell oscillations; we do not assign a
> global Fourier spectrum to unverified altermagnetic field scans. The few-electron thermal
> response is obtained from an exact fixed-particle-number canonical partition function, avoiding
> finite-system errors of a fixed-mean-number approximation. The circular model supports an
> equilibrium charge current but no persistent spin current.
