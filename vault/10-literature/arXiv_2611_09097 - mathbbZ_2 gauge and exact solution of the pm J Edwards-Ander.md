# $\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge and exact solution of the $\pm J$ Edwards-Anderson model in a transverse field

## Abstract

> Using the Majorana representation of spin-1/2 operators, we study the $\pm J$ Edwards--Anderson
> model in a transverse field at $T=0$. We show that, in this representation, the quantity
> describing the coupling between neighboring spins commutes with the Hamiltonian and, unlike in
> the Kitaev model, is not subject to any Gauss law. The resulting $\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge freedom
> allows the disorder in the signs of the exchange integrals to be eliminated by an exact gauge
> transformation, so that the spectrum of the system coincides with that of the model with uniform
> couplings. The resulting theory is quadratic in the Majorana fields. Computing the local Green's
> functions, we find that the Edwards--Anderson order parameter vanishes for $Δ>J\sqrt{Z}$ and
> equals $1/4$ for $Δ<J\sqrt{Z}$, which signals a second-order transition from a paramagnetic
> phase to a spin-glass ground state that is degenerate over spin configurations and replica
> symmetric at $T=0$.
