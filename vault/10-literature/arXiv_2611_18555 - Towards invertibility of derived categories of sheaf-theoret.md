# Towards invertibility of derived categories of sheaf-theoretic line operators

## Abstract

> We investigate when the braided monoidal $\infty$-categories of sheaf-theoretic line operators
> of $3$-dimensional topological quantum field theories are invertible in the higher Morita
> category, so that they determine invertible $4$-dimensional anomaly theories. We consider a zoo
> of different examples which are inherently derived. First, enhancing Ben-Zvi--Francis--Nadler,
> we show that a symmetric monoidal category of quasi-coherent sheaves $\mathrm{QC}(X)$ is
> invertible only if $X$ is a point, and convolution categories on loop stacks are Morita trivial
> whenever the stack satisfies descent along its diagonal, e.g. for classifying stacks. Secondly,
> we provide an example showing that, unlike for finite braided tensor categories, non-degeneracy
> does not imply invertibility in the derived setting: For an odd vector space $V[1]$, the
> $2$-shifted $\mathcal{D}$-modules $\mathrm{DMod}^2(V[1])$ are $3$-dualizable and non-degenerate
> but neither factorizable nor cofactorizable. This category is closely related to the line
> operators of linear Rozansky--Witten theory. Finally, we define an anomaly of a group action on
> an invertible braided monoidal $\infty$-category as a Witt class over $\mathrm{Rep}(G)$, and
> show that the category of representations of the quantum group of a torus at a root of unity is
> invertible relative to its quantum Frobenius.
