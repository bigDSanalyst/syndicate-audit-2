# On the (non-)closedness of the set of ReLU networks

## Abstract

> We prove that the set $\mathcal{N}_N$ of ReLU networks with one hidden layer and at most
> $N\in\mathbb N$ neurons is weakly closed in $W^{1,p}(Ω)$, for a bounded connected open set
> $Ω\subset\mathbb R^d$. Consequently, every coercive, weakly lower semicontinuous energy on this
> space attains its minimum over $\mathcal{N}_N$. This covers the Deep Ritz method with convex
> integrands, Sobolev training and Lipschitz learning. Moreover, as the weight decay parameter
> tends to zero, minimizers of the regularized problem converge, up to subsequences, to minimizers
> with minimal parameter norm. We also prove that the set of ReLU networks with two hidden layers
> of bounded widths is not closed in $W^{1,p}(Ω)$ or in $C(\overlineΩ)$. This disproves a
> conjecture of Petersen, Raslan and Voigtlaender.
