# SN 2026ahvu in NGC 1433 May Be a Prompt Type Ia Supernova with a Delay Time Under 100 Myr

## Abstract

> SN 2026ahvu is a Type Ia supernova (SN) that occurred in the nearby galaxy NGC 1433. Using
> archival Hubble and JWST imaging from the PHANGS survey, we show that the SN lies within
> 0.19$''$ and 0.26$''$ of two UV-bright star clusters. This $0.19/0.26''$ separation corresponds
> to $8/11$ pc ($17/24$ pc) at the reported distances of $9.1$ and $18.9$ Mpc, respectively. The
> probability of finding an unrelated Type Ia SN this close to a cataloged star cluster by chance
> is only $\sim$0.4 %. Thus, SN 2026ahvu is likely the first Type Ia SN that can be associated
> with relatively young star clusters in a nearby galaxy. From spectral energy distribution
> fitting using HST+JWST (or HST-only) data, we find that the clusters have age estimates of
> $37_{-5}^{+3}$ ($57_{-9}^{+14}$) Myr and $95_{-13}^{+15}$ ($88_{-12}^{+14}$) Myr. Association
> with either cluster would mean that SN 2026ahvu should be considered a prompt SN Ia and provide
> one of the most concrete measurements to date of a prompt Ia delay time. The median age of the
> younger star cluster is close to the minimum possible delay time for SN Ia progenitors. If the
> SN is indeed associated with the younger cluster, it would imply that Type Ia SNe can occur
> almost immediately after white dwarf formation. If the SN is associated with the older cluster,
> this discovery still presents the first example of a prompt Type Ia SN with delay time under 100
> Myr.
