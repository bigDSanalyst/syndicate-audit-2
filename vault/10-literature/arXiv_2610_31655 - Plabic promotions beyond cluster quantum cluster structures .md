# Plabic promotions beyond cluster: quantum cluster structures and the dual canonical basis

## Abstract

> Plabic tangles define geometric promotion maps between configurations and, after choosing a
> lift, between Grassmannians. The main conjecture in \cite{Tangles} is that when the tangle is
> dominant and solvable the induced algebraic promotion is cluster quasi-homomorphism. This work
> extends the cluster perspective in two directions. First, we initiate the study of quantum
> promotions. We conjecture that all promotions defined from dominant solvable tangles are
> naturally lifted to a \emph{quantum cluster quasi homomorphism}, and prove for all cases in the
> literature that the were shown to be (classical) cluster quasi-homomorphisms. Secondly, we
> define the notion of cluster-Lusztig quasi-homomorphisms, which are cluster quasi homomorphisms
> that take Lustig's dual canonical basis elements to dual canonical basis element, up to a
> Laurent monomial in frozen variables, with clearing and normalization chosen uniformly on each
> content component. We prove the unexpected result that the classical and quantum $m$-unary star
> promotions fall into this category, and conjecture that all promotions coming from dominant
> solvable tangles share the same property. We then consider promotions via non solvable cores of
> intersection number higher than one. We use it to study facets of amplituhedron cells, and to
> show that some of them do not lie in zero loci of dual canonical basis elements, disproving a
> conjecture by T. Lam \cite{LamGP}.
