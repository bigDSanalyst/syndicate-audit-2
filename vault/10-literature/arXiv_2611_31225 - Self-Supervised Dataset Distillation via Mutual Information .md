# Self-Supervised Dataset Distillation via Mutual Information Maximization

## Abstract

> Dataset distillation (DD) aims to compress a large dataset into a small synthetic one that
> remains effective for training neural networks. Despite its success in supervised learning, DD
> remains underexplored in self-supervised learning (SSL), where distilled data must preserve the
> information needed to learn representations that remain consistent across augmentations. To
> bridge this gap, we introduce Mutual Information Prototype Alignment (MIPA), an efficient SSL
> dataset distillation approach that optimizes synthetic images to preserve augmentation-invariant
> information. Specifically, MIPA treats synthetic images as learnable prototypes and uses a
> frozen encoder to assign real samples to them based on feature similarity. A synthetic dataset
> that preserves such augmentation invariance should yield stable prototype assignments across
> multiple augmented views of the same real image. MIPA encourages this by maximizing mutual
> information between cross-view assignments while avoiding trivial solutions in which all real
> samples are assigned to only a few synthetic prototypes. Evaluations on image classification
> benchmarks show that synthetic data generated by MIPA yield higher-quality representations than
> existing distillation approaches while reducing time and storage demands during distillation.
